# Eonian — Dimmu Borgir Drum Setup (Daray, 2018)

## Overview

*Eonian* (released May 4, 2018 through Nuclear Blast) was Dimmu Borgir's first studio album in nearly eight years, and only the second time Daray's drumming was captured on a Dimmu Borgir studio record following *Abrahadabra* (2010). Recorded at Fascination Street Studios in Örebro, Sweden between January and April 2017, the album was self-produced by Dimmu Borgir with mixing and mastering handled by Jens Bogren — one of extreme metal production's most in-demand engineers, known for his work across Opeth, Kreator, and Arch Enemy.

The long gap between records raised the stakes on every performance decision, and Daray's drum tracking had to deliver the same orchestral-scale precision that had defined the band's studio catalog since *Death Cult Armageddon* (2003). *Eonian* continued Dimmu Borgir's pattern of pairing extreme metal drumming with large-scale orchestral and choral arrangements, requiring the same tempo discipline and dynamic control that had carried Daray through *Abrahadabra* eight years earlier.

By *Eonian*, Daray's rig had changed since his studio debut: he switched from the Pearl Reference Pure kit heard on *Abrahadabra* to a Tama Starclassic Performer B/B kit in Piano Black in 2014, debuting the brand at the TAMA 40th Anniversary Drum Festival that July. The Paiste RUDE and 2002 cymbals and the Pearl Demon Drive double pedal that had anchored his first Dimmu Borgir session in 2010 carried over unchanged across that brand switch, giving *Eonian* a rig that was part new foundation, part decade-plus continuity. This article breaks down that setup as it was applied to *Eonian*'s sessions at Fascination Street Studios, and how Daray's core technique translated across a mid-tenure kit change.

## Gear Breakdown

- **Drums:** Tama Tama Starclassic Performer B/B, Piano Black (Piano Black finish)
- **Snare:** Tama Tama S.L.P. Black Brass 14" x 6.5" (LBR1465), 14" x 6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Paiste
- **Heads:** Evans Ambassador Coated (batter) — controlled attack with warm fundamental
- **Snare tuning:** Medium-high tension for fast response and upper-frequency cut against dense orchestral/choral arrangements

### Tama Starclassic Performer B/B: Daray's Kit Since 2014, On Record for the First Time

Daray brought his Tama Starclassic Performer B/B kit into *Eonian*'s sessions at Fascination Street Studios — the first time this kit was captured on a Dimmu Borgir studio record. He had switched to Tama in 2014, four years after tracking *Abrahadabra* on a Pearl Reference Pure kit, debuting the Starclassic Performer B/B in Piano Black at the TAMA 40th Anniversary Drum Festival that July. By the time *Eonian*'s sessions began in January 2017, the kit had already been road-tested across several years of international touring.

The birch/bubinga hybrid shell construction gave Jens Bogren's mix the consistent, uniform resonance needed to sit alongside *Eonian*'s dense orchestral and choral arrangements without the tonal drift that lower-grade shells develop under sustained extreme-tempo striking. The double 22" kick configuration continued to anchor Dimmu Borgir's rhythmic foundation, with the Starclassic Performer B/B's response giving each kick strike consistent attack timing — essential given the album's layered production, where kick patterns needed to lock precisely against the surrounding orchestral and choral tracking at Fascination Street.

The Piano Black finish carried the same theatrical integration into *Eonian*'s touring cycle that the kit's Pearl predecessor had provided, keeping Daray's rig visually consistent with Dimmu Borgir's stage production even as the underlying hardware brand changed.

### Snare: Tama S.L.P. Black Brass Under Jens Bogren's Production

Daray's brass-shell Tama S.L.P. Black Brass snare — part of the Starclassic Performer B/B kit he switched to in 2014 — gave *Eonian* the same bright, fast-decaying crack that has defined his sound throughout his Dimmu Borgir tenure — necessary once again on a record where orchestral and choral arrangements occupy much of the same frequency territory the snare needs to cut through. Under Jens Bogren's mix at Fascination Street Studios, the snare's medium-high tuning and defined attack kept every hit distinct against the album's dense production, consistent with the same acoustic requirement that has governed Daray's snare choice across both his Pearl and Tama eras.

### Paiste RUDE and 2002: Unchanged Through Eight Years of Touring

By *Eonian*, Daray's Paiste RUDE and 2002 cymbal combination had been road-tested across nearly a decade of international touring behind *Abrahadabra* — durability that the RUDE series' thick construction was specifically built to deliver. That same setup returned for the *Eonian* sessions, giving the album's dynamic range the same bright, cutting projection through Jens Bogren's dense production that it had provided on Daray's studio debut.

The 14" Sound Edge Hi-Hats' open, shimmering character continued to serve *Eonian*'s more orchestrated passages, integrating with the choral and keyboard arrangements without the stiff, dry closed sound of a conventional hi-hat pairing.

## Eonian: A New Kit, the Same Technical Foundation

*Eonian* captures Daray on different core gear than his studio debut: by the January 2017 sessions he had been playing a Tama Starclassic Performer B/B kit in Piano Black since 2014, replacing the Pearl Reference Pure kit heard on *Abrahadabra* eight years earlier. What carried across that switch — the Paiste RUDE/2002 cymbals, the Pearl Demon Drive pedal, the Vic Firth sticks, the Evans heads — mattered as much as what changed, anchoring his performance at Fascination Street Studios under Jens Bogren's production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What drum kit did Daray use on Eonian?**

A: Daray played a Tama Starclassic Performer B/B kit in Piano Black on Eonian (2018) — a switch he made in 2014, four years after tracking Abrahadabra (2010) on a Pearl Reference Pure kit. The double 22" kick configuration and birch/bubinga hybrid shells gave Jens Bogren's production the consistent response needed against the album's dense orchestral and choral arrangements at Fascination Street Studios. For his complete gear breakdown, see the [Daray drum setup guide](/articles/daray-drum-setup).

**Q: Was Eonian Daray's second studio album with Dimmu Borgir?**

A: Yes. Daray joined Dimmu Borgir in 2008, recorded Abrahadabra (2010) as his first studio album with the band, and then Eonian (2018) as his second — an eight-year gap during which Dimmu Borgir toured extensively without releasing new studio material.

**Q: Where was Eonian recorded?**

A: Eonian was recorded at Fascination Street Studios in Örebro, Sweden between January and April 2017, and released May 4, 2018 through Nuclear Blast. The band self-produced the album, with Jens Bogren handling mixing and mastering.

**Q: What cymbals does Daray play on Eonian?**

A: Daray's Eonian cymbal setup is the same Paiste RUDE and 2002 combination he has used throughout his Dimmu Borgir tenure: 14" Sound Edge Hi-Hats, 16" RUDE and 18" 2002 crashes, a 22" RUDE Power Ride, and a 2002 China — road-tested across nearly a decade of touring between Abrahadabra and Eonian.
