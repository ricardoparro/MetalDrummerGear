## Overview

Released on August 9, 2011 on Roadrunner Records, "In Waves" marked a deliberate pivot for Trivium — away from the sprawling, progressive ambition of "Shogun" (2008) and back toward direct, riff-forward songwriting. The gamble paid off commercially: the album debuted at #13 on the Billboard 200, Trivium's best chart placement to that point, and gave the band their first UK top-20 entry.

Nick Augusto joined Trivium in 2010 following the departure of Travis Smith, and In Waves was his studio debut with the band. The album needed tight, aggressive, radio-ready drumming that still carried a thrash-metal edge, and tracks like "In Waves," "Built to Fall," and "Watch the World Burn" showcase Augusto stamping his own technical, thrash-rooted identity on the band's sound from his first record forward.

Recorded at Audiohammer Studios in Sanford, Florida with producers Jason Suecof and Mark Lewis, the sessions produced Trivium's heaviest-sounding record since "The Crusade." Augusto's relentless double-bass work and punchy snare attack became the rhythmic backbone of the band's commercial breakthrough, setting up the even bigger chart success of "Vengeance Falls" two years later.

This article breaks down the specific gear Nick Augusto used on In Waves — his Pearl Reference Pure kit and Sabian AAX cymbal setup.

## Gear Breakdown

- **Drums:** Pearl Pearl Reference Pure (Custom finish finish)
- **Snare:** Pearl Pearl Reference 14"x6.5" Brass, 14" x 6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Sabian — Sabian AAX Series
- **Heads:** Evans Genera Dry (batter), Evans 300 Snare Side (resonant)
- **Snare tuning:** Medium-high — balanced crack and body for a direct, riff-forward backbeat

### Augusto's Pearl Reference Pure

For his studio debut, Augusto's Reference Pure was configured as a compact four-piece kit — a single 22" kick, two rack toms, and two floor toms. The configuration reflects the album's songwriting: In Waves calls for tight, riff-doubling patterns rather than extended melodic tom fills, and the lean setup was built to match.

The maple/birch hybrid shell construction of the Reference Pure delivered a fast attack with controlled low-end resonance. Birch contributes a faster attack and more high-frequency clarity, while the maple layer keeps enough low-end warmth to project through the album's heavier, more compressed production. It's a kit built for punch rather than tonal complexity.

On the In Waves World Tour (2011–2012), Augusto occasionally expanded to a double-kick configuration for extended live double-bass passages, giving him low-end weight without the latency a double pedal can introduce at speed. The Reference Pure's high-tension lugs kept shell vibration contained even at full touring volume.

### The In Waves Crack: Pearl Reference Brass

The Pearl Reference brass snare at 14" x 6.5" is the sound of the In Waves backbeat — bright, immediate, and tuned to cut cleanly through Jason Suecof's dense, drop-tuned guitar mix.

Tuned medium-high, the snare delivers clean rimshots on the album's aggressive sections while retaining enough body on the backbeat to avoid thinning out over a full set. That balance of crack and thud is central to the album's rhythmic identity on tracks like "In Waves" and "Black."

### Sabian AAX: A Bright, Cutting Voice

In Waves carried forward the same bright, cutting Sabian AAX setup Augusto used throughout his Trivium tenure — a voice well matched to the album's overall shift from progressive texture to direct impact. The AAX X-Plosion Crashes at 18" and 19" speak immediately on accent hits, cutting through the album's dense guitar saturation, while the AAX 14" Stage Hi-Hats articulate cleanly at the fast 16th-note tempos the record's double-bass sections demand.

The 18" AAXtreme China above the floor tom became the go-to effect cymbal for the band's breakdown entrances and riff-change hits — a short-decay, aggressive voice suited to In Waves' more direct song structures.

## In Waves: The Sound of Trivium's Breakthrough

In Waves is the record that introduced Nick Augusto as Trivium's drummer — his studio debut with the band after joining in 2010. The gear tells the story of that debut:

- **Pearl Reference Pure**: A compact four-piece configuration built for tight, punchy attack on In Waves' direct, riff-forward songwriting
- **Pearl Reference 14"x6.5" Brass Snare**: A bright, cutting crack tuned for direct, riff-forward songwriting rather than orchestrated dynamics
- **Sabian AAX Cymbals**: The bright, cutting cymbal voice Augusto relied on throughout his Trivium tenure, built for cutting through a heavier, more compressed mix

The result was Trivium's best chart performance to that point — a #13 Billboard 200 debut and the band's first UK top-20 entry — and set the stage for the even bigger commercial success of Vengeance Falls two years later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What kit does Nick Augusto use on In Waves?**

A: On Trivium's In Waves (2011), Nick Augusto played a Pearl Reference Pure kit with maple/birch hybrid shells, configured with a 22" bass drum, two rack toms (10" and 12"), and two floor toms (14" and 16") — a compact four-piece configuration built for the album's tight, direct songwriting. He paired the kit with a Pearl Reference brass snare and Sabian AAX cymbals. For the complete drummer profile, see [Nick Augusto at MetalForge](/drummer/nick-augusto).

**Q: Did Nick Augusto play on Trivium's Shogun?**

A: No. Nick Augusto joined Trivium in 2010, two years after Shogun's 2008 release. Shogun was recorded by Trivium's drummer at the time, Travis Smith. In Waves (2011) was Augusto's studio debut with the band, followed by Vengeance Falls (2013). For Augusto's own gear breakdown, see [In Waves drum setup at MetalForge](/articles/in-waves-drum-setup).

**Q: What is Nick Augusto's overall drum setup?**

A: Nick Augusto's core setup across his Trivium tenure (2010–2014) centered on Pearl drums, Sabian cymbals, and Vic Firth sticks. On In Waves (2011) he played a compact four-piece Pearl Reference Pure kit with Sabian AAX cymbals, a setup he carried into Vengeance Falls (2013). For the full In Waves gear breakdown, see [In Waves drum setup at MetalForge](/articles/in-waves-drum-setup).

**Q: How did In Waves perform commercially for Trivium?**

A: In Waves debuted at #13 on the Billboard 200 upon its August 2011 release — Trivium's best chart placement at the time — and gave the band their first entry into the UK Albums Chart top 20. It served as the commercial bridge between the more progressive, critically acclaimed Shogun (2008) and the even bigger chart success of Vengeance Falls (2013), which reached #15 on the Billboard 200 and cracked the top 10 in Germany and Finland.
