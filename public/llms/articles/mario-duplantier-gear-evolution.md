# Mario Duplantier Gear Evolution — Gojira Drum Kit History

## Overview

Mario Duplantier's gear evolution is one of the most dramatic in modern metal. Over nearly three decades with Gojira, Mario progressed from budget Pearl Export kits in Bayonne club venues to a Tama Starclassic Bubinga rig engineered for stadium-scale performances alongside Metallica. Each transition in his setup was driven by the music — from the raw aggression of *Terra Incognita* through the atmospheric precision of *L'Enfant Sauvage* to the globally celebrated productions of *Magma* and *Fortitude*.

What makes Mario's evolution significant is the consistency of his dual bass drum philosophy throughout. From his earliest recordings, two independent kick drums were non-negotiable — not a double pedal, but two separate instruments, each resonating independently. That commitment shaped every endorsement decision and every gear transition across his career.

This timeline documents every major era: the drums, cymbals, pedals, and heads that powered each stage of Gojira's ascent from French underground to Download Festival headliners.

## Era 1: Terra Incognita Era (1996–2004)

**Kit:** Pearl Export Series (poplar shells, dual 22" kicks, 10"/12"/13"/16" toms)
**Snare:** Pearl Sensitone Steel 14"×5.5"
**Cymbals:** Paiste 2002 (14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride, 18" China)
**Pedals:** Pearl P-2002C Eliminator double pedal
**Heads:** Remo Ambassador (clear on toms, coated on snare)

Gojira (originally Godzilla) built their sound in Bayonne on whatever Mario could access. The Pearl Export was the natural choice for a developing extreme metal band — affordable, reliable, and widely available across Europe. Mario prioritized the dual kick configuration from the beginning, establishing the tribal tom-pattern vocabulary that would define his playing across the entire Gojira catalog.

The Paiste 2002 cymbals — the standard for European extreme metal of the era — gave Mario the cutting power needed to project over Gojira's dense, down-tuned guitar tones in club environments. The Eliminator's cam-driven mechanism helped him achieve the fluid dual bass patterns on early tracks like "Remembrance" and "Satan is a Lawyer."

*Albums:* [Terra Incognita (2001) drum setup](/articles/terra-incognita-drum-setup) · The Link (2003)

## Era 2: From Mars to Sirius Era (2005–2008)

**Kit:** Pearl Export Series (poplar shells, dual 22" kicks, 10"/12"/13"/16" toms)
**Snare:** Pearl Sensitone Steel 14"×5.5"
**Cymbals:** Paiste 2002 (14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride, 18" China)
**Pedals:** Pearl P-2002C Eliminator double pedal
**Heads:** Remo Ambassador (clear on toms, coated on snare)

*From Mars to Sirius* (2005) was the album that put Gojira on the global metal map, but Mario had no drum endorsement yet — the same Pearl Export and Paiste 2002 setup from the Terra Incognita years carried the massive tom fills on "Flying Whales." The dual kick configuration and tribal tom vocabulary established in the formative years remained unchanged through this breakthrough and *The Way of All Flesh* (2008), which earned Gojira their first Grammy nomination.

*Albums:* [From Mars to Sirius drum setup](/articles/from-mars-to-sirius-drum-setup) · [The Way of All Flesh drum setup](/articles/the-way-of-all-flesh-drum-setup)

## Era 3: L'Enfant Sauvage Era (2009–2015)

**Kit:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga (bubinga shells, dual 22"×18" kicks, 8"/10"/12"/13"/16"/18" toms)
**Snare:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga 14"×6.5" (bubinga shell)
**Cymbals:** Zildjian K Custom / A Custom / Z Custom (14" hi-hats, 17"/18"/19" crashes, 21" ride, 18" China)
**Pedals:** Tama Iron Cobra double pedal
**Heads:** Evans G2 (toms), Evans EMAD (kicks)

*L'Enfant Sauvage* (2012) demanded Mario's most versatile performance — blast beats and delicate atmospheric passages within the same track. Mario signed with Tama in 2010, switching to the Starclassic Bubinga kit and a Zildjian K Custom / A Custom / Z Custom cymbal mix that gave him the tonal nuance needed for the album's atmospheric passages while still cutting through the heavier material.

Mario added a smaller 8" rack tom for higher melodic range, expanding the configuration to its largest form. He also switched to Evans heads for better attack definition — the EMAD kick heads' focused, punchy character improved the dual bass clarity in both studio and live contexts. The Tama Iron Cobra double pedal offered improved response and consistency across longer festival sets.

*Albums:* [L'Enfant Sauvage drum setup](/articles/lenfant-sauvage-drum-setup)

## Era 4: Magma / Fortitude Era (2016–Present)

**Kit:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga (bubinga shells, dual 22"×18" kicks, 10"/12"/13"/16"/18" toms)
**Snare:** Tama S.L.P. 14"×6.5" G-Maple
**Cymbals:** Zildjian K Custom / A Custom / Z Custom (14" hi-hats, 17"/18"/19" crashes, 21" ride, 18" China)
**Pedals:** Tama Iron Cobra 900 Power Glide (dual independent pedals)
**Sticks:** Tama Mario Duplantier Signature
**Heads:** Ahead drumheads (batter surfaces)

Gojira's rise to stadium-level production demanded a corresponding refinement in Mario's gear. The Tama Starclassic Bubinga kit's exotic bubinga shells deliver added density and projection for stadium and festival stages while retaining the warmth and fundamental tone that distinguishes great drums from merely loud ones. Mario switched to the Tama S.L.P. G-Maple snare alongside it for extra body and crack in the dense Gojira mix.

The Tama Iron Cobra 900 Power Glide replaced the earlier Iron Cobra double pedal for dual bass — chosen for the Power Glide cam's fluid, controlled motion at demanding live tempos. Tama also developed the Mario Duplantier Signature stick around his aggressive attack and tribal pattern requirements. The Zildjian K/A/Z Custom cymbal partnership continued, its dark, complex tones suiting the dynamic range on *Magma* and *Fortitude*.

This era saw Gojira headline Download Festival, open for Metallica at stadiums, and perform at the Paris Olympics 2024 opening ceremony — the definitive confirmation that Mario's refined Starclassic Bubinga rig operates at the highest level in world music.

*Albums:* [Magma drum setup](/articles/magma-drum-setup) · [Fortitude drum setup](/articles/fortitude-drum-setup)

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Mario Duplantier use on From Mars to Sirius?**
Mario Duplantier used his existing Pearl Export kit on *From Mars to Sirius* (2005) — he had no drum endorsement yet. The configuration featured dual 22" poplar-shell kick drums, plus 10"/12"/13"/16" toms. He paired them with Paiste 2002 cymbals and a Pearl P-2002C Eliminator double pedal, the same setup that carried Gojira's international breakthrough. See the full [From Mars to Sirius drum setup](/articles/from-mars-to-sirius-drum-setup) for complete details.

**Q: What drum kit did Mario Duplantier use on Magma?**
Mario recorded and toured *Magma* (2016) with his Tama Starclassic Bubinga kit — dual 22"×18" kicks in bubinga shells, 10"/12"/13"/16"/18" toms. Cymbals were Zildjian K Custom / A Custom / Z Custom throughout. Pedals were Tama Iron Cobra 900 Power Glide (dual independent units). This is the same fundamental configuration he used on *Fortitude* (2021) and continues to use through the current era. See the [Magma drum setup](/articles/magma-drum-setup) for the full breakdown.

**Q: Why did Mario Duplantier switch from Paiste 2002 to Zildjian?**
The switch from Paiste 2002 to Zildjian K Custom / A Custom / Z Custom came during the *L'Enfant Sauvage* era (2009–2015), alongside Mario's 2010 Tama signing. The 2002's bright, cutting character served early Gojira's more aggressive club-era material perfectly, but *L'Enfant Sauvage* required more dynamic nuance — atmospheric passages that demanded darker, more complex cymbal tones. The Zildjian K/A/Z Custom mix's layered tonal character responds to dynamic variation in ways the brighter 2002 line does not. The Zildjian partnership has continued through *Magma*, *Fortitude*, and beyond.

**Q: Has Mario Duplantier always used two bass drums?**
Yes — from Gojira's earliest recordings, Mario has used two independent kick drums rather than a double pedal. The dual kick configuration (typically dual 22" shells) has been consistent across the Pearl Export era and the current Tama Starclassic Bubinga era. Two physical kick drums give independent resonance from each shell, a different playing feel for each foot, and the visual impact that matches Gojira's cinematic stage presentation. This commitment shaped every endorsement decision across his career.

**Q: What sticks does Mario Duplantier use?**
Mario Duplantier currently uses the Tama Mario Duplantier Signature sticks — his first signature stick deal, developed during the Magma/Fortitude era. Tama designed the model around his aggressive attack and tribal pattern requirements, featuring a heavier taper and larger bead than standard models. In earlier eras (Terra Incognita through L'Enfant Sauvage), Mario used standard Vic Firth 5B sticks without a signature endorsement.
