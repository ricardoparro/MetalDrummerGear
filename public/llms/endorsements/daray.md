# Daray — Brand Endorsements

**Drummer:** Daray
**Band:** Dimmu Borgir
**Profile:** https://metalforge.io/drummers/daray/endorsements

---

## Current Endorsements

### Drums: Tama

Daray endorses Tama for drums. They play the Tama Starclassic Performer B/B, Piano Black (snare: S.L.P. Black Brass LBR1465). This partnership began in 2014.

### Cymbals: Paiste

Daray endorses Paiste for cymbals. They play the Paiste RUDE / 2002 Series. This partnership began in 2008.

### Drumsticks: Vic Firth

Daray endorses Vic Firth for drumsticks. They play the Vic Firth American Classic Extreme 5B. This partnership began in 2008.

### Drumheads: Evans

Daray endorses Evans for drumheads. They play the Evans Emperor / Ambassador. This partnership began in 2008.

### Hardware / Pedals: Pearl

Daray endorses Pearl for hardware / pedals. They play the Pearl Demon Drive Double Bass Pedal. This partnership began in 2010.

## Endorsement History

- **2008** (Drums): Signed with Pearl — Replaced Hellhammer in Dimmu Borgir and brought his Pearl Reference Pure kit (custom black finish, double 22" kick) into the band, first tracked on studio debut "Abrahadabra" (2010)
- **2008** (Cymbals): Signed with Paiste — Built his setup around Paiste RUDE and 2002 series cymbals (15" Sound Edge Hi-Hats, 22" RUDE Power Ride) for durability and projection across Dimmu Borgir's festival-scale live shows
- **2010** (Hardware / Pedals): Signed with Pearl — Used the Pearl Demon Drive chain-drive double pedal on his Dimmu Borgir studio debut "Abrahadabra" (2010), recorded alongside the Kringkastingsorkestret orchestra and Schola Cantorum choir
- **2014** (Drums): Switched from Pearl to Tama — Switched to a Tama Starclassic Performer B/B kit in Piano Black, with a 14"x6.5" S.L.P. Black Brass snare (LBR1465); date is best-available, anchored to his appearance at the TAMA 40th Anniversary Drum Festival on July 5, 2014

## FAQ

**Q: What brands does Daray endorse?**
A: Daray endorses Tama, Paiste, Vic Firth, Evans, Pearl. Their primary drum endorsement is Tama and they play Paiste cymbals.

**Q: Does Daray have a signature drum or cymbal?**
A: Daray is a key Tama endorser but does not have a dedicated signature kit model in the current lineup.
