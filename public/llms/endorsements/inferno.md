## Current Endorsements

### Drums: Pearl

Inferno endorses Pearl for drums. They play the Pearl Reference Series. This partnership began in 2000s.

### Cymbals: Paiste

Inferno endorses Paiste for cymbals. They play the Paiste RUDE Series. This partnership began in 2000s.

### Drumsticks: Vic Firth

Inferno endorses Vic Firth for drumsticks. They play the Vic Firth American Classic Extreme 5B. This partnership began in 2000s.

### Drumheads: Remo

Inferno endorses Remo for drumheads. This partnership began in 2000s.

### Hardware / Pedals: Monolit

Inferno endorses Monolit for hardware / pedals. They play the Monolit Czarcie Kopyto. This partnership began in 2010s.
