# John Longstreth — Brand Endorsements

**Drummer:** John Longstreth
**Band:** Origin
**Profile:** https://metalforge.io/drummers/john-longstreth/endorsements

## Current Endorsements

### Drums: Pearl

John Longstreth endorses Pearl for drums. This partnership began in 1990s.

### Cymbals: Meinl

John Longstreth endorses Meinl for cymbals. They play the Meinl Classics Custom Dark Series. This partnership began in 2000s.

### Drumsticks: Scorpion Percussion

John Longstreth endorses Scorpion Percussion for drumsticks. They play the Scorpion Percussion "Wee Heavy" Signature. This partnership began in 2010s.

### Hardware / Pedals: ACD Unlimited

John Longstreth endorses ACD Unlimited for hardware / pedals. They play the ACD Unlimited Bass Drum Pedals + FootBlaster Triggers. This partnership began in 2010s.

## Signature Models

- Scorpion Percussion "Wee Heavy" Signature Drumsticks (2010)

## Endorsement History

- **1995** (Drums): Signed with Pearl — Played Pearl drums from Angelcorpse's 1995 formation through his ongoing tenure in Origin, recording "Informis Infinitas Inhumanitas" (2002) and "Chaosmos" (2022)
- **2010** (Cymbals): Signed with Meinl — Signed with Meinl Cymbals, per the brand's own artist page — Classics Custom Dark series (8" Splash, 14" Hi-Hats, 18" China) plus a Generation X 12"/14" Trash Hat
- **2010** (Drumsticks): Signature product: Scorpion Percussion "Wee Heavy" Signature Drumsticks — Scorpion Percussion credits him as a signature artist for its "Wee Heavy" stick model on the brand's own artist page
