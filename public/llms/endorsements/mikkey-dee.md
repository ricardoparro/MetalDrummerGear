# Mikkey Dee — Brand Endorsements

**Drummer:** Mikkey Dee
**Band:** Scorpions / Motörhead
**Profile:** https://metalforge.io/drummers/mikkey-dee/endorsements

## Current Endorsements

### Drums: Sonor

Mikkey Dee endorses Sonor for drums. They play the Sonor SQ2. Mikkey Dee has a signature model: the Mikkey Dee Signature Snare.

### Cymbals: Paiste

Mikkey Dee endorses Paiste for cymbals. They play the Paiste Signature Series. This partnership began in 1987.

### Drumsticks: Wincent

Mikkey Dee endorses Wincent for drumsticks. They play the Wincent Mikkey Dee Signature. Mikkey Dee has a co-designed signature product with Wincent.

### Drumheads: Evans

Mikkey Dee endorses Evans for drumheads.

### Hardware / Pedals: DW

Mikkey Dee endorses DW for hardware / pedals. They play the DW 5000 Series Double Pedal.

## Signature Models

- Sonor Mikkey Dee Signature Snare
- Wincent Mikkey Dee Signature (signature)

## Endorsement History

- **1992** (Drums): Signed with Tama — Joined Motörhead playing a Tama Artstar II/Swingstar kit with Paiste cymbals, first heard on "March ör Die" and "Bastards"
- **2012** (Drums): Signature product: Sonor  — Released his Sonor Mikkey Dee Signature 14"x7.25" birch snare, part of the Sonor SQ2 setup he's used since 2012
- **2016** (Drums): Renewed Sonor deal — Carried his longtime Sonor SQ2 and Paiste Signature setup into Scorpions after joining the band in 2016 following Lemmy Kilmister's death

## FAQ

A: Mikkey Dee endorses Sonor, Paiste, Wincent, Evans, DW. Their primary drum endorsement is Sonor and they play Paiste cymbals.
