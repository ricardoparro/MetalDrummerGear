## Current Endorsements

### Drums: Pearl

Nick Augusto endorses Pearl for drums. They play the Pearl Reference Pure.

### Cymbals: Sabian

Nick Augusto endorses Sabian for cymbals. They play the Sabian AAX Series.

### Drumsticks: Pro-Mark

Nick Augusto endorses Pro-Mark for drumsticks. They play the Pro-Mark Nylon Tip 5B.
