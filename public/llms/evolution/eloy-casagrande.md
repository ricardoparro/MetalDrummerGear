# Eloy Casagrande Drum Kit Evolution — Complete Timeline

Eloy Casagrande's career arc is the most compelling in modern metal drumming: a Brazilian teenager who joined Sepultura at 16, spent a decade building one of the most technically accomplished and distinctive setups in heavy music, and then landed the most high-profile drum role available — replacing Jay Weinberg in Slipknot in 2023. His gear evolution documents a journey from his first Tama kit in Sepultura to the stadium-scale rig he now plays on Knotfest stages worldwide.

## Sepultura Debut Era (2011–2016)

**Albums:** The Mediator Between Head and Hands Must Also Be the Conductor (2013)
**Tours:** The Mediator World Tour 2013–2014; South American and European dates

Eloy Casagrande joined Sepultura in 2011 at just 16 years old — one of the most remarkable early-career signings in metal history. A teenager stepping into a band with a 25-year legacy and a catalog including Chaos A.D. and Roots demanded immediate professional-grade performance. His Tama endorsement began with the band, giving him the reliable foundation needed for his first years of sustained international touring — and he was already a Paiste artist, having signed with the cymbal brand in 2005 at age 17.

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga — 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Tama Bell Brass 14"×5.5" (BB146) — bright, cutting attack for Sepultura's thrash/groove metal material
- **Cymbals:** Paiste — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride, 18" China
- **Hardware:** Tama Iron Cobra Double Pedal (chain-drive for controlled aggression)
- **Sticks:** Promark hickory (standard, early endorsement period)
- **Heads:** Evans double-ply on toms (durability for intensive touring as a teenage professional)
- **Estimated kit cost (original):** ~$8,000

**Key developments:**
- Joined Sepultura at age 16 (2011) — one of metal's most remarkable early signings
- Tama Starclassic Bubinga endorsement begins immediately with the band
- Already a Paiste artist since 2005, six years before joining Sepultura
- The Mediator Between Head and Hands (2013) announces his arrival as a world-class metal drummer

> "Being in Sepultura at 16 was a dream. I had to grow up fast — the music and the touring demanded everything from me immediately." — *Modern Drummer, 2014*

## Machine Messiah / Quadra Era (2017–2022)

**Albums:** Machine Messiah (2017), Quadra (2020), SepulQuarta (2021)
**Tours:** Machine Messiah World Tour; Quadra World Tour 2020–2022

The era where Eloy Casagrande fully came into his own as a defining drummer of the modern metal generation. Machine Messiah (2017) showcased a dramatically more sophisticated musical palette — combining Sepultura's Brazilian rhythmic identity with progressive metal structures. Quadra (2020) — a conceptual double album exploring Jungian psychology — is considered the high-water mark of his Sepultura work: technically demanding, dynamically varied, and musically ambitious. His setup expanded significantly to match these compositional ambitions.

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga — 22" kick, 8"/10"/12"/14"/16" toms *(upgrade — expanded configuration for broader tonal palette across Quadra's varied dynamics from acoustic passages to full brutality)*
- **Snare:** Tama Bell Brass 14"×5.5" (BB146) *(continued — consistent snare choice across entire Sepultura tenure)*
- **Cymbals:** Paiste — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18"/19" crashes, 21" ride, 18" China *(upgrade — expanded setup for Quadra's dynamic range)*
- **Hardware:** Tama Iron Cobra Double Pedal *(continued — proven double bass performance for the album's technical demands)*
- **Sticks:** Promark hickory
- **Heads:** Evans on toms, Evans kick head *(upgrade — focused, punchy low-end on heavier material)*
- **Electronics:** Roland SPD-SX Sampling Pad *(new — electronics integrated for Quadra's layered studio arrangements in the live context)*
- **Estimated kit cost (original):** ~$14,000

**Key developments:**
- Expanded Tama Starclassic Bubinga configuration for broader tonal range on Quadra
- Paiste cymbal setup expanded for Quadra's varied material
- Evans kick heads introduced for more focused low-end punch
- Roland SPD-SX integrated for live electronics during Quadra touring
- Quadra (2020) widely recognised as the defining achievement of his Sepultura career

> "Quadra gave me the chance to show every side of what I can do. Aggressive, melodic, dynamic — I had to be all of it." — *Revolver Magazine, 2020*

## Slipknot Era (2023–Present)

**Albums:** Upcoming Slipknot studio material
**Tours:** Slipknot World Tour 2024; Knotfest worldwide

The most dramatic transition in recent metal drumming history. In late 2023, Eloy Casagrande joined Slipknot — replacing Jay Weinberg and stepping into one of the most high-profile drum roles in all of heavy music. The move from Sepultura's underground credibility to Slipknot's stadium-scale productions represents a seismic shift, but Eloy maintained his existing endorsements rather than switching brands: his current setup still runs Tama drums, Paiste cymbals, and Evans heads, now scaled for the demands of performing in front of tens of thousands.

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga (dual bass drum configuration) — 22"×16" & 24"×14" bass drums, 10", 12", 13" toms, 16" & 18" floor toms *(upgrade — expanded dual-kick setup for stadium-level dynamics)*
- **Snare:** Tama Bell Brass 14"×5.5" (BB146) *(continued — the snare that has followed Eloy from Sepultura to Slipknot)*
- **Cymbals:** Paiste (expanded configuration) — 15" Masters Dark hi-hats, 20" Masters Dark ride, dual 20" 602 crashes, 10" Rude splash, 20" Masters Dark crash, 20" 2002 Heavy ride, 20" 2002 Novo China, 10" 2002 Mega Bell, Symphonic Gong *(upgrade — expanded to cover the full dynamic range of Slipknot's catalog)*
- **Hardware:** Tama Iron Cobra Double Pedal *(continued — absolute reliability required for Slipknot's production scale)*
- **Sticks:** Promark Eloy Casagrande Signature *(signature — custom model launched to coincide with the Slipknot chapter)*
- **Heads:** Evans on toms, Evans on kick *(upgrade — tighter, more defined low-end in Slipknot's dense arena mix)*
- **Electronics:** Roland TD module + SPD-SX *(upgrade — full hybrid setup for Slipknot's layered production and sample-heavy live show)*
- **Estimated kit cost (original):** ~$25,000

**Key developments:**
- Joined Slipknot (2023) — replaced Jay Weinberg in one of metal's most iconic drum roles
- Maintained existing Tama, Paiste, and Evans endorsements rather than switching brands for the new chair
- Promark Eloy Casagrande signature sticks launched coinciding with the Slipknot chapter
- Expanded Paiste cymbal configuration for Slipknot's catalog breadth
- Roland TD module + SPD-SX for Slipknot's full hybrid production requirements
- Stadium-scale debut at Knotfest events worldwide

> "Slipknot is something else entirely. The stage, the production, the catalog — I had to absorb everything and make it my own from day one." — *Metal Hammer Interview, 2024*

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit does Eloy Casagrande use with Slipknot?**
A: Eloy Casagrande plays a Tama Starclassic Bubinga kit with Slipknot — a dual bass drum configuration with 22"×16" and 24"×14" bass drums, 10", 12", 13" toms and 16" and 18" floor toms. He continues his long-standing Tama endorsement from his Sepultura years, with the dual-kick setup representing an upgrade to match Slipknot's stadium productions.

**Q: When did Eloy Casagrande join Slipknot?**
A: Eloy Casagrande joined Slipknot in 2023, replacing Jay Weinberg as the band's drummer. He made his first live appearances with Slipknot on their 2024 world tour and at Knotfest events, becoming the band's first non-American drummer in a core lineup role.

**Q: What kit did Eloy Casagrande use with Sepultura?**
A: During his Sepultura tenure (2011–2022), Eloy played a Tama Starclassic Bubinga kit, expanding the configuration over the years to suit increasingly dynamic material like Quadra (2020). He used a Tama Bell Brass snare and Paiste cymbals throughout, with the Tama Iron Cobra double pedal across the entire tenure.

**Q: What cymbals does Eloy Casagrande use?**
A: Eloy Casagrande has been a Paiste cymbal artist since 2005 — six years before even joining Sepultura — and has carried the partnership through his entire Sepultura and Slipknot career. His current Slipknot setup includes an expanded Paiste configuration covering Masters Dark and 2002 series cymbals, hi-hats, multiple crashes, two rides, China, splash, and a symphonic gong to cover the full dynamic range of the band's catalog.

**Q: How old was Eloy Casagrande when he joined Sepultura?**
A: Eloy Casagrande was born on July 27, 1994, and joined Sepultura in 2011 — making him approximately 16 or 17 years old at the time. His debut album with the band, The Mediator Between Head and Hands Must Also Be the Conductor, was released in 2013.

**Q: What drumsticks does Eloy Casagrande use?**
A: Eloy Casagrande plays Promark Eloy Casagrande Signature drumsticks — a custom model launched around the time of his Slipknot debut in 2023. He previously used standard Promark hickory sticks during his Sepultura tenure before the signature model was developed.

**Q: What makes Eloy Casagrande the standout modern metal drummer?**
A: Eloy Casagrande's story combines technical excellence with a narrative arc that has no peer in contemporary metal: Brazilian teenager → decade-long artistic development with Sepultura → replacing the successor to Joey Jordison in Slipknot. His Quadra work with Sepultura (2020) demonstrated compositional maturity beyond his years, and his Slipknot transition at age 28 represents one of the most dramatic career pivots in heavy music history.
