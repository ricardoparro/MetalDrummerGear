## Founding Kublai Khan TX & Youth War Era (2009–2014)

**Albums:** Kublai Khan TX: Youth War EP (2010)
**Tours:** Texas and regional DIY hardcore shows (2009–2014)

Lamb co-founded beatdown hardcore band Kublai Khan in Sherman, Texas in 2009 alongside vocalist Matt Honeycutt, self-releasing the "Youth War" EP in 2010 to establish the band's crushing, breakdown-driven sound. As the only drummer in the band's history, Lamb built his powerful, groove-oriented style playing regional Texas hardcore shows during this DIY period, for which no single fixed drum, cymbal, or stick endorsement is publicly documented.
