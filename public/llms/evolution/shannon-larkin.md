# Shannon Larkin Drum Kit Evolution — Complete Timeline

Shannon Larkin began his professional drumming career in the late 1980s with Maryland thrash/glam band Wrathchild America, then spent the first half of the 1990s in Ugly Kid Joe — best known for the worldwide hit "Everything About You" — before joining Godsmack in 2002. In Godsmack he found his long-term home, anchoring the band's platinum-selling run from 2002 until his 2024 retirement on a ddrum Dios Series kit, paired with a Sabian cymbal setup that progressed from HHX to an HHX/AAX mix over the years, a DW 9000 Series double pedal, and Vic Firth American Classic 5B sticks. Larkin retired from Godsmack in 2024; Mike Mangini (ex-Dream Theater) became the band's drummer in 2026.

## Wrathchild America / Ugly Kid Joe Era (1989–2001)

**Albums:** Ugly Kid Joe – America's Least Wanted (1992), Ugly Kid Joe – Menace to Sobriety (1995)
**Tours:** Regional and club touring with Wrathchild America, national touring behind Ugly Kid Joe's early-'90s run

Before Godsmack, Shannon Larkin built his career in Maryland's Wrathchild America (1989–1991) and then Ugly Kid Joe (1991–1997), whose "Everything About You" became a worldwide hit off the multi-platinum *America's Least Wanted*. ## Faceless Era (2002–2007)

**Albums:** Faceless (2003), IV (2006)
**Tours:** Faceless Tour, Ozzfest 2003, IV World Tour

Larkin joined Godsmack in 2002, replacing Tommy Stewart, and immediately recorded *Faceless* — one of the band's most commercially successful albums. He brought his ddrum Dios Series kit into the band's platinum-selling run, and its punchy, focused low end became one of rock radio's most recognizable drum sounds. Ozzfest 2003 introduced him to rock's largest audiences.

- **Drums:** ddrum Dios Series — 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms *(new — brought into Godsmack when he joined in 2002)*
- **Snare:** ddrum Dios 14"×6.5" Maple *(new)*
- **Cymbals:** Sabian HHX Series — 14" HHX hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 21" ride, 18" china *(new — signed with Sabian upon joining Godsmack)*
- **Hardware:** DW 9000 Series Double Pedal *(new — signed with DW upon joining Godsmack)*
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B *(new — non-signature hickory, signed upon joining Godsmack)*
- **Heads:** Evans G2 / EMAD *(new)*
- **Estimated kit cost (original):** ~$9,000

**Key developments:**
- Joined Godsmack (2002) — career-defining move after pre-Godsmack years in Wrathchild America and Ugly Kid Joe
- ddrum Dios Series endorsement begins
- Sabian HHX, Vic Firth American Classic 5B, and DW 9000 Series endorsements begin
- Ozzfest 2003 brought massive national exposure
- *Faceless* snare tone became one of rock radio's most recognizable drum sounds
- Formed side project Another Animal (2007) with Godsmack bassist Robbie Merrill

## The Oracle / 1000hp Era (2009–2015)

**Albums:** The Oracle (2010), 1000hp (2014)
**Tours:** The Oracle World Tour, 1000hp Tour

Godsmack's commercial peak. *The Oracle* was the band's most ambitious album to that point, with a wider melodic range and more nuanced arrangements — but Larkin's ddrum Dios Series kit, unchanged since he joined the band, suited the evolved sound without needing a change.

- **Drums:** ddrum Dios Series — 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms, custom finish *(unchanged from Faceless era)*
- **Snare:** ddrum Dios 14"×6.5" Maple *(unchanged)*
- **Cymbals:** Sabian HHX / AAX Series Mix — 14" HHX hi-hats, 16"/18" AAX crashes, 21" ride, 18" china *(upgrade — mixed in AAX for a wider tonal palette)*
- **Hardware:** DW 9000 Series Double Pedal *(unchanged)*
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B *(unchanged)*
- **Heads:** Evans G2 / EMAD2 *(upgrade — EMAD2 for tighter kick response)*
- **Estimated kit cost (original):** ~$14,000

**Key developments:**
- ddrum Dios Series kit continues unchanged from the Faceless era
- Sabian HHX/AAX mix for a wider cymbal palette, Vic Firth and DW 9000 Series setup remains consistent
- Evans EMAD2 upgrade for tighter, more focused kick attack on 1000hp
- The Oracle World Tour — Godsmack's largest production to that point

> "The ddrum kit just works. It breathes. When we were making The Oracle, I needed that warmth — it was a different kind of record, but the gear didn't need to change." — *Modern Drummer Interview, 2010*

## When Legends Rise / Modern Era (2016–2024)

**Albums:** When Legends Rise (2018), Lighting Up the Sky (2023)
**Tours:** When Legends Rise Tour, Lighting Up the Sky Tour 2023

Larkin's most refined and critically praised era, and his last with the band. His ddrum Dios Series kit and Sabian AAX/HHX cymbal setup anchored the raw power and groove-centric approach behind Godsmack's continued arena-level touring. Roland electronics were added for consistent live quality on major touring productions. *Lighting Up the Sky* (2023) was Larkin's final studio album with Godsmack before he announced his retirement from the band in 2024.

- **Drums:** ddrum Dios Series — 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms, custom finishes *(unchanged)*
- **Snare:** ddrum Dios 14"×6.5" Maple *(unchanged)*
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AAX & HHX Series — 14" AAX Stage hi-hats, 18"/19" AAX X-Plosion crashes, 21" HHX Raw Bell Dry ride, 10" AAX splash, 18" AAX china *(mix of AAX and HHX)*
- **Hardware:** DW 9000 Series Double Pedal, ddrum Throne *(unchanged pedal; throne added)*
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B (non-signature)
- **Heads:** Evans G2 / EMAD2 (decade-long consistent configuration)
- **Electronics:** Roland SPD-SX Sampling Pad *(new — click track and ambient sample integration)*
- **Estimated kit cost:** ~$18,000

**Key developments:**
- Long-standing ddrum Dios Series kit continues to anchor Godsmack's sound
- Sabian AAX/HHX mix, Vic Firth, and DW 9000 Series setup remains consistent since 2002
- Roland SPD-SX introduced for arena-quality live consistency
- *Lighting Up the Sky* (2023) — Larkin's final studio album with Godsmack
- Announced his retirement from Godsmack in 2024; Mike Mangini (ex-Dream Theater) became the band's drummer in June 2026, after interim touring drummers Will Hunt and Wade Murff

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Shannon Larkin play with Godsmack?**
A: Larkin played a ddrum Dios Series kit with custom finishes — 22" kick drum and 10", 12", 14", 16" toms. He played this kit from joining Godsmack in 2002 until his 2024 retirement from the band.

**Q: What cymbals did Shannon Larkin use?**
A: Larkin's Sabian cymbal setup progressed from HHX (2002–2007) to an HHX/AAX mix (2009–2015) to an AAX & HHX mix (2016–2024). He played Sabian from joining Godsmack in 2002 until his 2024 retirement.

**Q: What double pedal did Shannon Larkin use?**
A: Larkin played a DW 9000 Series double pedal from joining Godsmack in 2002 until his 2024 retirement — he never switched pedal brands.

**Q: What sticks did Shannon Larkin play?**
A: Larkin played Vic Firth American Classic 5B drumsticks — a standard, non-signature hickory model he used from joining Godsmack in 2002 until his 2024 retirement.

**Q: When did Shannon Larkin join Godsmack?**
A: Larkin joined Godsmack in 2002, replacing Tommy Stewart. His first album with the band was *Faceless* (2003). He had previously played in Wrathchild America (1989–1991) and Ugly Kid Joe (1991–1997) during a pre-Godsmack career spanning over a decade.

**Q: Is Shannon Larkin still Godsmack's drummer?**
A: No. Larkin announced his retirement from Godsmack in 2024, having recorded every studio album from *Faceless* (2003) through *Lighting Up the Sky* (2023). Mike Mangini (ex-Dream Theater) became Godsmack's drummer in June 2026, following interim touring drummers Will Hunt and Wade Murff.
