# Shannon Larkin Drum Kit Evolution — Complete Timeline

Shannon Larkin began his professional drumming career in the late 1980s with Maryland thrash/glam band Wrathchild America, then spent the first half of the 1990s in Ugly Kid Joe — best known for the worldwide hit "Everything About You" — before joining Godsmack in 2002. In Godsmack he found his long-term home, anchoring the band's platinum-selling run on a ddrum Dios Series kit that has remained his setup for over two decades, paired with Sabian AAX cymbals, a DW 9000 Series double pedal, and Vic Firth American Classic 5B sticks — none of which have changed since he joined.

## Wrathchild America / Ugly Kid Joe Era (1989–2001)

**Albums:** Ugly Kid Joe – America's Least Wanted (1992), Ugly Kid Joe – Menace to Sobriety (1995)
**Tours:** Regional and club touring with Wrathchild America, national touring behind Ugly Kid Joe's early-'90s run

Before Godsmack, Shannon Larkin built his career in Maryland's Wrathchild America (1989–1991) and then Ugly Kid Joe (1991–1997), whose "Everything About You" became a worldwide hit off the multi-platinum *America's Least Wanted*. No specific drum-gear endorsement from this pre-Godsmack period is independently verified — his documented brand partnerships (ddrum, Sabian, Vic Firth, DW, Evans) all begin in 2002 upon joining Godsmack.

- **Hardware:** No fixed brand independently verified for this period
- **Sticks:** No fixed brand independently verified for this period

**Key developments:**
- Began professional career in Wrathchild America (1989–1991)

- Released *Menace to Sobriety* with Ugly Kid Joe (1995)

## Faceless Era (2002–2007)

**Albums:** Faceless (2003), IV (2006)
**Tours:** Faceless Tour, Ozzfest 2003, IV World Tour

Larkin joined Godsmack in 2002, replacing Tommy Stewart, and immediately recorded *Faceless* — one of the band's most commercially successful albums. He brought his ddrum Dios Series kit into the band's platinum-selling run, and its punchy, focused low end became one of rock radio's most recognizable drum sounds. Ozzfest 2003 introduced him to rock's largest audiences.

- **Drums:** ddrum Dios Series — 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms *(new — brought into Godsmack when he joined in 2002)*
- **Snare:** ddrum Dios 14"×6.5" Maple *(new)*
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AAX Series — 14" hi-hats, 18"/19" crashes, 21" AAX ride, 10" splash, 18" china *(new — signed with Sabian upon joining Godsmack)*
- **Hardware:** DW 9000 Series Double Pedal *(new — signed with DW upon joining Godsmack)*
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B *(new — non-signature hickory, signed upon joining Godsmack)*
- **Heads:** Evans G2 / EMAD *(new)*
- **Estimated kit cost (original):** ~$9,000

**Key developments:**
- Joined Godsmack (2002) — career-defining move after pre-Godsmack years in Wrathchild America and Ugly Kid Joe
- ddrum Dios Series endorsement begins
- Sabian AAX, Vic Firth American Classic 5B, and DW 9000 Series endorsements begin
- Ozzfest 2003 brought massive national exposure
- *Faceless* snare tone became one of rock radio's most recognizable drum sounds
- Formed side project Another Animal (2007) with Godsmack bassist Robbie Merrill

## The Oracle / 1000hp Era (2009–2015)

**Albums:** The Oracle (2010), 1000hp (2014)
**Tours:** The Oracle World Tour, 1000hp Tour

Godsmack's commercial peak. *The Oracle* was the band's most ambitious album to that point, with a wider melodic range and more nuanced arrangements — but Larkin's ddrum Dios Series kit, unchanged since he joined the band, suited the evolved sound without needing a change.

- **Drums:** ddrum Dios Series — 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms, custom finish *(unchanged from Faceless era)*
- **Snare:** ddrum Dios 14"×6.5" Maple *(unchanged)*
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AAX Series — 14" hi-hats, 18"/19" crashes, 21" AAX ride, 10" splash, 18" china *(unchanged)*
- **Hardware:** DW 9000 Series Double Pedal *(unchanged)*
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B *(unchanged)*
- **Heads:** Evans G2 / EMAD2 *(upgrade — EMAD2 for tighter kick response)*
- **Estimated kit cost (original):** ~$14,000

**Key developments:**
- ddrum Dios Series kit continues unchanged from the Faceless era
- Sabian AAX, Vic Firth, and DW 9000 Series setup remains consistent
- Evans EMAD2 upgrade for tighter, more focused kick attack on 1000hp
- The Oracle World Tour — Godsmack's largest production to that point

> "The ddrum kit just works. It breathes. When we were making The Oracle, I needed that warmth — it was a different kind of record, but the gear didn't need to change." — *Modern Drummer Interview, 2010*

## When Legends Rise / Modern Era (2016–Present)

**Albums:** When Legends Rise (2018), Lighting Up the Sky (2023)
**Tours:** When Legends Rise Tour, Lighting Up the Sky Tour 2023

Larkin's most refined and critically praised era. His ddrum Dios Series kit and Sabian AAX cymbal setup — unchanged since 2002 — continue to anchor the raw power and groove-centric approach behind Godsmack's continued arena-level touring. Roland electronics were added for consistent live quality on major touring productions.

- **Drums:** ddrum Dios Series — 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms, custom finishes *(unchanged)*
- **Snare:** ddrum Dios 14"×6.5" Maple *(unchanged)*
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AAX Series — 14" hi-hats, 18"/19" crashes, 21" AAX ride, 10" splash, 18" china *(unchanged)*
- **Hardware:** DW 9000 Series Double Pedal *(unchanged)*
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B (current, non-signature)
- **Heads:** Evans G2 / EMAD2 (decade-long consistent configuration)
- **Electronics:** Roland SPD-SX Sampling Pad *(new — click track and ambient sample integration)*
- **Estimated kit cost:** ~$18,000

**Key developments:**
- Long-standing ddrum Dios Series kit continues to anchor Godsmack's sound
- Sabian AAX, Vic Firth, and DW 9000 Series setup remains consistent since 2002
- Roland SPD-SX introduced for arena-quality live consistency
- *Lighting Up the Sky* (2023) — Godsmack's most recent studio album, career continuing strong

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit does Shannon Larkin currently play?**
A: Larkin plays a ddrum Dios Series kit with custom finishes — 22" kick drum and 10", 12", 14", 16" toms. He has played this same kit since joining Godsmack in 2002.

**Q: What cymbals does Shannon Larkin use?**
A: Larkin plays Sabian AAX Series cymbals — 14" hi-hats, 18" and 19" crashes, a 21" AAX ride, a 10" splash, and an 18" china. He has played Sabian since joining Godsmack in 2002.

**Q: What double pedal does Shannon Larkin use?**
A: Larkin plays a DW 9000 Series double pedal, which he has used since joining Godsmack in 2002 — he has never switched pedal brands.

**Q: What sticks does Shannon Larkin play?**
A: Larkin plays Vic Firth American Classic 5B drumsticks — a standard, non-signature hickory model he has used since joining Godsmack in 2002.

**Q: When did Shannon Larkin join Godsmack?**
A: Larkin joined Godsmack in 2002, replacing Tommy Stewart. His first album with the band was *Faceless* (2003). He had previously played in Wrathchild America (1989–1991) and Ugly Kid Joe (1991–1997) during a pre-Godsmack career spanning over a decade.
