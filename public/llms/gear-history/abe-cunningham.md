## Gear Timeline

### Founding / Early Deftones Era (1988–1994)

- **Drums:** ddrum Dios Series — 22" kick, 12"/13"/16" toms
- **Snare:** ddrum Steel 14"x5.5"
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian A Series — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride
- **Pedal:** Various budget hardware, single bass pedal
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth 5B
- **Notable:** Cunningham co-founded Deftones in Sacramento with Chino Moreno, Stephen Carpenter, and Chi Cheng while still in high school, playing raw, hard-hitting drums on budget gear through the local club circuit.

### Adrenaline / Around the Fur Era (1995–1999)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic — maple shells; 22" kick, 10"/12"/13"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Tama Starclassic 14"x6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian A Custom Series — 14" A Custom hi-hats, 16"/18" A Custom crashes, 21" A Custom ride
- **Pedal:** Tama Iron Cobra 900 Rolling Glide double pedal
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark
- **Notable:** Deftones broke into the mainstream with Adrenaline (1995) and Around the Fur (1997); Cunningham signed with Tama, Zildjian, and Pro-Mark in 1997, debuting the Tama Starclassic kit on Around the Fur — relationships that have lasted his entire career — while Ozzfest and Warped Tour exposure drove his shift to a double-kick configuration.

### White Pony Era (2000–2003)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic — maple shells; 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Tama Starclassic Brass 14"x6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian K Series — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride
- **Pedal:** Tama Iron Cobra 900 Rolling Glide double pedal
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark
- **Original setup cost (1997):** ~$6,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$11,500
- **Notable:** White Pony (2000) is Deftones' critically acclaimed, Grammy-winning masterpiece. Cunningham stayed on the Tama Starclassic kit he'd signed in 1997 — no drum brand change accompanied the album — while switching from Zildjian A Custom to the darker Zildjian K Series, marking a decisive shift from nu-metal aggression toward atmospheric alt-metal sophistication.

### Self-Titled / Saturday Night Wrist Era (2003–2010)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic — maple shells; 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Tama Starclassic 14"x6.5" Maple
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian K Custom Series — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 21" ride
- **Pedal:** Tama Iron Cobra 900 Rolling Glide double pedal
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark
- **Notable:** Through the self-titled Deftones (2003) and the turbulent Saturday Night Wrist (2006) sessions, Cunningham stayed on his Tama Starclassic kit while refining his cymbal voice to Zildjian K Custom. Chi Cheng's near-fatal accident (November 2008) cast a shadow over the band heading into what would become Diamond Eyes.

### Diamond Eyes Era (2010–2012)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic — maple shells; 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Tama Starclassic 14"x6.5" Maple
- **Cymbals:** Sabian HHX Series — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 21" ride
- **Pedal:** Tama Iron Cobra 900 Rolling Glide double pedal
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark
- **Notable:** Diamond Eyes (2010) — written and recorded rapidly in the wake of Chi Cheng's accident — showed Cunningham at his most focused and groove-driven, still on the same Tama Starclassic kit. He moved to a Sabian HHX cymbal setup for the album, carrying that darker, more focused voice through the next decade of Deftones records.

### Koi No Yokan / Gore / Ohms Era (2012–2022)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga (Egyptian Night Mist) — maple/bubinga shells; 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Tama Starclassic matching snare (no separate model publicly documented)
- **Cymbals:** Sabian HHX Series — 14" hi-hats, 18"/20" crashes, 22" ride, 19" China
- **Pedal:** Tama Iron Cobra Power Glide Twin Pedal (HP900PTW), Iron Cobra Lever Glide Hi-Hat (HH905)
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark
- **Heads:** Remo Powerstroke P3 / Pinstripe / Ambassador
- **Electronics:** Roland SPD-SX sampling pad
- **Notable:** Cunningham settled into a Tama Starclassic Bubinga kit and carried the Sabian HHX cymbal setup through Koi No Yokan (2012), Gore (2016), and Ohms (2020) — the band's most compositionally sophisticated modern work.

### Current Verified Setup — Zildjian Return Era (2022–present)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga (Egyptian Night Mist)
- **Snare:** Tama Starclassic matching snare (no separate model publicly documented)
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian A Custom & K Custom hybrid — 14" K Custom Hi-Hats, 18"/20" K Custom Crashes, 22" K Custom Ride, 19" A Custom China
- **Pedal:** Tama Iron Cobra Power Glide Twin Pedal (HP900PTW), Tama hardware
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark
- **Heads:** Remo
- **Notable:** Cunningham's current verified rig pairs his Tama Starclassic Bubinga kit with a Zildjian A Custom/K Custom cymbal spread — returning to Zildjian in 2022 after twelve years on Sabian HHX — dynamic, groove-first restraint that has defined nearly four decades behind the Deftones kit.

## Key Gear Changes

- **1997:** ddrum → Tama Starclassic — signed with Tama, Zildjian, and Pro-Mark; debuted on Around the Fur
- **~2000:** Zildjian A Custom → K Series — darker, more atmospheric cymbal tone for White Pony
- **~2003:** Zildjian K → K Custom — more refined tonal palette
- **2010:** Zildjian K Custom → Sabian HHX — groove-forward voice for Diamond Eyes
- **~2012:** Tama Starclassic (maple) → Tama Starclassic Bubinga — Koi No Yokan era
- **2022:** Sabian HHX → Zildjian A Custom/K Custom hybrid — returned to Zildjian after twelve years
- **Career constant:** Tama drum endorsement, Pro-Mark sticks, and Remo heads, maintained continuously since the 1997 signing

## FAQ

**Q: What is Abe Cunningham's drum setup worth?**  
A: Abe Cunningham's original 1997 Tama Starclassic setup — debuted on Deftones' Around the Fur — cost approximately $6,000. Adjusted for 2026 inflation, that's equivalent to roughly $11,500. His current verified Tama Starclassic Bubinga setup, with a Zildjian A Custom/K Custom cymbal spread and Tama hardware, represents a modern equivalent worth approximately $18,500, or roughly $19,500 adjusted for inflation.

**Q: What drums did Abe Cunningham use on White Pony?**  
A: On White Pony (2000), Cunningham played the same Tama Starclassic kit he'd signed with in 1997 for Around the Fur — no drum brand change accompanied the album. He did switch from Zildjian A Custom to the darker Zildjian K Series during this era, a tonal shift that matched the album's atmospheric, dynamic direction.

**Q: What drums does Abe Cunningham play now?**  
A: Abe Cunningham's current verified kit is a Tama Starclassic Bubinga setup (Egyptian Night Mist finish) with a matching Tama Starclassic snare, a Zildjian A Custom & K Custom cymbal spread, a Tama Iron Cobra Power Glide Twin Pedal, and Pro-Mark sticks.

**Q: When did Abe Cunningham join Deftones?**  
A: Abe Cunningham is a founding member of Deftones — he co-founded the band in Sacramento, California in 1988 with Chino Moreno, Stephen Carpenter, and the late Chi Cheng, and has appeared on every studio album since.

**Q: What cymbals does Abe Cunningham use?**  
A: Cunningham has been a Zildjian artist since signing in 1997 (alongside Tama and Pro-Mark), progressing from the A Series through A Custom, K Series, and K Custom. He moved to a Sabian HHX setup for Diamond Eyes (2010) through Ohms (2020), then returned to a Zildjian A Custom/K Custom hybrid in 2022 — reflecting a decades-long pursuit of tones suited to Deftones' atmospheric sound.
