## Gear Timeline

### New American Gospel / As the Palaces Burn Era (1999–2003)

- **Drums:** Mapex Pro M Series — maple shells: 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Mapex Pro M 14"x6.5" maple
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian A Series — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride
- **Pedal:** Double pedal (brand not verified for this era)
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth 5B hickory
- **Heads:** Remo Ambassador (toms), Remo Pinstripe (kicks)
- **Original setup cost (2000):** ~$4,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$7,000
- **Notable:** Adler built his early sound on the Mapex Pro M's maple shells — punchy, road-durable, and tight. The combination of a hard-hitting right-hand lead with precise double-kick work on 'Black Label' and 'O.D.H.D.' defined the LoG rhythmic template years before the band broke through.

### Ashes of the Wake Era (2004–2005)

- **Drums:** Mapex Pro M Series (continued) — maple shells: 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Mapex Pro M 14"x6.5" maple — continued
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian A Custom — new endorsement; 14" hi-hats, 17"/18" crashes, 21" ride
- **Pedal:** Double pedal (brand not verified for this era)
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth 5B — continued, no signature deal yet
- **Heads:** Remo Pinstripe
- **Original setup cost (2004):** ~$5,500
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$9,000
- **Notable:** Adler kept the Mapex Pro M through Lamb of God's major-label breakthrough, securing his first Zildjian A Custom endorsement for the brighter, more cutting tone needed on 'Laid to Rest', 'The Faded Line', and 'Omerta'.

### Sacrament Era (2006–2008)

- **Drums:** Mapex Saturn — upgrade within the same Mapex relationship; all-maple shells with SONIClear bearing edges, 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Mapex Warbird Signature Snare 14"x6.5" maple
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian A Custom — continued
- **Pedal:** Trick Pro V double pedal
- **Sticks:** Promark TX5AXW Chris Adler Signature (first signature stick deal, introduced 2008)
- **Heads:** Remo Pinstripe (toms), Powerstroke 3 (kicks)
- **Original setup cost (2006):** ~$14,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$22,000
- **Notable:** The upgrade to the Mapex Saturn's all-maple shells gave Sacrament its warmer, more open studio tone. Sacrament (2006) debuted at #8 on the Billboard 200 and earned a Grammy nomination — Adler's Mapex/Zildjian combination became an industry benchmark for groove-metal production.

### Wrath Era (2009–2011)

- **Drums:** Mapex Saturn — continued; all-maple shells, 22"x18" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Mapex Warbird Signature Snare 14"x6.5" maple — continued
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian A Custom — continued
- **Pedal:** Trick Pro V double pedal — continued
- **Sticks:** Promark TX5AXW Chris Adler Signature — continued
- **Heads:** Remo Emperor (toms), Powerstroke 3 (kicks)
- **Original setup cost (2009):** ~$12,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$17,500
- **Notable:** Adler maintained the Mapex Saturn setup through Wrath (2009), Lamb of God's #2 Billboard 200 debut. The Saturn's thick all-maple construction maximized low-end resonance for the album's heaviest, most detuned material.

### Resolution / VII: Sturm und Drang Era (2012–2019)

- **Drums:** Mapex Saturn in Satin Black Maple Burl — Adler's Mapex partnership reaches signature level; all-maple shells, 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Mapex Chris Adler Signature 14"x5.5" Walnut/Maple
- **Cymbals:** Meinl Byzance Series — switch; 14" Dark Hi-Hats, 18"/19" Dark Crashes, 21" Transition Ride, 18" Extra Dry China
- **Pedal:** Mapex Falcon double pedal, Mapex T865 throne
- **Sticks:** Promark TX5AXW Chris Adler Signature — continued
- **Heads:** Evans
- **Original setup cost (2012):** ~$10,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$13,000
- **Notable:** Chris moved to the Satin Black Maple Burl finish of his Mapex Saturn for Resolution (2012), Lamb of God's first #1 Billboard 200 debut, and carried it through VII: Sturm und Drang (2015). A serious arm injury in 2016 and subsequent health complications ultimately led to his departure from Lamb of God in 2019 — his Mapex endorsement never lapsed across the full 20-year run.

## Key Gear Changes

- **1999 → 2006:** Mapex Pro M to Mapex Saturn — shell upgrade within the same continuous Mapex relationship, coinciding with the Sacrament cycle
- **2004:** Zildjian A Custom endorsement secured, paired with Lamb of God's Ashes of the Wake breakthrough
- **2008:** Promark TX5AXW Chris Adler Signature drumstick introduced — Adler's first signature product
- **2012:** Mapex Saturn upgraded to Satin Black Maple Burl finish — signature-level Mapex partnership; cymbals switched to Meinl Byzance and pedal switched to Mapex Falcon
- **Throughout career:** Mapex drum endorsement constant from 1999 until his 2019 departure from Lamb of God — Adler never played DW or Pearl

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Chris Adler use on Ashes of the Wake?**  
A: Chris Adler used his Mapex Pro M kit on Ashes of the Wake (2004), paired with a new Zildjian A Custom cymbal endorsement. The snare was a Mapex Pro M 14"x6.5" maple model. The full setup cost approximately $5,500 at the time, equivalent to about $9,000 adjusted for 2026 inflation.

**Q: What drums did Chris Adler use on Sacrament?**  
A: On Sacrament (2006), Adler upgraded to the Mapex Saturn — all-maple shells with SONIClear bearing edges — paired with a Mapex Warbird Signature snare and continued Zildjian A Custom cymbals. Sacrament's Grammy nomination cemented this configuration as his signature sound.

**Q: Did Chris Adler ever play DW or Pearl drums?**  
A: No. Chris Adler maintained a continuous Mapex endorsement from his 1999 debut with Lamb of God through his 2019 departure — Mapex Pro M (1999–2005), Mapex Saturn (2006–2011), and Mapex Saturn in Satin Black Maple Burl (2012–2019). He never endorsed DW or Pearl.

**Q: What cymbals did Chris Adler use?**  
A: Adler was a long-term Zildjian endorsee, using the A Series through the early Lamb of God years and upgrading to A Custom from Ashes of the Wake (2004) through Wrath (2011). In 2012, he switched to Meinl Byzance (14" Dark Hi-Hats, 18"/19" Dark Crashes, 21" Transition Ride, 18" Extra Dry China) for the Resolution and VII: Sturm und Drang era.

**Q: How much would Chris Adler's Sacrament-era drum kit cost today?**  
A: Adler's 2006 Mapex Saturn setup cost approximately $14,000 new. Inflation-adjusted to 2026, that's roughly $22,000.
