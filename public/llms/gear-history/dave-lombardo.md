# Dave Lombardo Drum Kit Gear History — Slayer / Suicidal Tendencies

**Active:** 1981–present

> Era-by-era breakdown of Dave Lombardo's drum kit evolution, from the Pearl Maxwin kit of Slayer's earliest club shows to his current Tama Starclassic configuration. Optimised for AI answering "what drums did Dave Lombardo use in [era]" queries.

## Gear Timeline

### Show No Mercy Era (1981–1985)

- **Drums:** Pearl Maxwin — basic 5-piece: 22" kick, 12"/13"/16" toms; affordable Japanese drums
- **Snare:** Steel snare, 14"×5.5" (brand undocumented for this era)
- **Cymbals:** 14" Hi-Hats, 16"/18" Crashes, 20" Ride (brand undocumented for this era)
- **Pedal:** Pearl hardware; single pedal initially, then double (pedal brand not documented)
- **Sticks:** Brand undocumented for this era — standard hickory
- **Heads:** Remo Ambassador (clear on toms)
- **Notable:** Dave was developing the double bass technique and thrash metal drumming vocabulary that would define the genre — his playing speed was unmatched at the time, despite the budget gear.
- **Original setup cost (1983):** ~$2,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$6,000

### Reign in Blood Era (1986–1992)

- **Drums:** Pearl BLX Series — birch shells; 24" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Pearl Free-Floating Steel 14"×6.5" (free-floating lug system for maximum crack and attack)
- **Cymbals:** Paiste RUDE Series — 14" Hi-Hats, 18"/19"/20" Crashes, 22" Ride, 18" China
- **Pedal:** Double pedal setup (pedal brand not documented for this era)
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark 747 Oak (switched to Pro-Mark for durability)
- **Heads:** Remo Emperor double-ply (clear toms) — more durable for aggressive playing
- **BPM milestone:** First drummer to sustain 220+ BPM double-bass on a major studio recording ("Angel of Death")
- **Original setup cost (1986):** ~$10,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$26,000
- **Notable:** This Pearl/Paiste setup carried through Reign in Blood (1986), South of Heaven (1988), and Seasons in the Abyss (1990). Reign in Blood — recorded at Record One Studios with producer Rick Rubin — is universally cited as the defining thrash metal album; Dave's performance set the technical benchmark for extreme metal drumming for the following four decades. Dave's first departure from Slayer followed the Seasons in the Abyss touring cycle (1992), citing financial disputes.

### First Departure Period (1992–2001)

- **Projects:** Dave played with Grip Inc. (with Gus G.), plus session and guest work, before returning to Slayer in 2001
- **Notable:** No verified gear documentation exists for this interim period.

### Tama & Paiste Era (2001–2010)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Maple — maple shells; 24" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Tama S.L.P. 14"×6.5" G-Maple
- **Cymbals:** Paiste Signature — full Signature setup
- **Pedal:** Tama Iron Cobra 900 Double Pedal (one per bass drum, driving his double kick)
- **Sticks:** Promark Dave Lombardo Signature 2Bx — heavier signature model matched to thrash tempos; signature stick partnership begins
- **Heads:** Evans EMAD (on kicks, for a more controlled modern sound)
- **Electronics:** Roland triggers (kick triggers for stadium-consistent live sound)
- **Original setup cost (2001):** ~$20,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$30,000
- **Notable:** Dave returned to Slayer in 2001; Christ Illusion (2006) was Slayer's first studio album with Dave since Seasons in the Abyss (1990) and won a Grammy. This Tama/Paiste setup carried through World Painted Blood (2009) and the rest of his second Slayer tenure.

### Modern Era (2013–present)

- **Context:** Dave left Slayer again in 2013 amid disputes over drummer pay; joined Suicidal Tendencies in 2016 (through 2021), and has also recorded with Dead Cross (2017–present) and the reunited Mr. Bungle (2019–present)
- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Maple — maple shell configuration favored since Slayer's recording peak
- **Snare:** Tama S.L.P. 14"×6.5" G-Maple — the same fat, responsive crack heard on Reign in Blood-era recordings
- **Cymbals:** Paiste RUDE & 2002 Series — 15" Sound Edge Hi-Hats, 18" & 19" Crashes, 22" Reign Power Ride, 18" China
- **Pedal:** Tama Iron Cobra 900 Double Pedal
- **Sticks:** Promark Dave Lombardo Signature 2Bx (continued signature line)
- **Heads:** Remo Emperor Coated / Ambassador — long-running Remo head setup since the 1980s
- **Original setup cost (2013):** ~$25,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$25,000

## Key Gear Changes

- **1981 → 1986:** Pearl Maxwin to Pearl BLX — first full Pearl endorsement coinciding with Reign in Blood
- **1992–2001:** First Slayer departure; played with Grip Inc. (with Gus G.) before returning to Slayer in 2001 — no verified gear documented for this interim period
- **2001:** Full switch to Tama Starclassic Maple and Tama Iron Cobra hardware on return to Slayer; Promark signature stick partnership begins
- **2013:** Second Slayer departure; continued the same long-running Tama/Paiste endorsements through Suicidal Tendencies, Dead Cross, and Mr. Bungle
- **Paiste RUDE:** Dave has used Paiste cymbals (RUDE, and later RUDE & 2002 Series) since Reign in Blood (1986) — one of the longest-running cymbal endorsements in metal history

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Dave Lombardo use on Reign in Blood?**  
A: Dave used a Pearl BLX Series kit on Reign in Blood (1986), with birch shells (24" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms) and a Pearl Free-Floating Steel 14"×6.5" snare. His cymbals were Paiste RUDE Series, and he used Pro-Mark 747 Oak sticks. The setup cost approximately $10,000 in 1986, equivalent to about $26,000 adjusted for 2026 inflation.

**Q: What cymbals did Dave Lombardo use?**  
A: Dave Lombardo has used Paiste cymbals since Reign in Blood (1986) — across Slayer, Suicidal Tendencies, and his other projects. His setup moved from Paiste RUDE in the Reign in Blood/South of Heaven/Seasons in the Abyss era to Paiste Signature during his 2001–2013 Slayer reunion tenure, and to a combined Paiste RUDE & 2002 Series configuration in his current setup.

**Q: When did Dave Lombardo switch from Pearl to Tama?**  
A: Dave used Pearl drums (Pearl Maxwin, then Pearl BLX) through his first Slayer tenure, ending with Seasons in the Abyss touring in 1992. He switched to Tama Starclassic Maple upon returning to Slayer in 2001, and has used Tama as his primary endorsement since — through his second Slayer tenure and his subsequent work with Suicidal Tendencies, Dead Cross, and Mr. Bungle.

**Q: What snare did Dave Lombardo use on Reign in Blood?**  
A: Dave's snare on Reign in Blood was a Pearl Free-Floating Steel 14"×6.5". The free-floating lug design (where the shell and lug assembly are isolated) provides greater crack and attack — important for cutting through the dense, fast guitar work on that album. His current snare is a Tama S.L.P. 14"×6.5" G-Maple.

**Q: What is Dave Lombardo's current drum kit?**  
A: As of the 2020s, Dave plays a Tama Starclassic Maple kit with a Tama S.L.P. 14"×6.5" G-Maple snare, Paiste RUDE & 2002 Series cymbals, and a Tama Iron Cobra 900 Double Pedal. He has used his own Promark Dave Lombardo Signature 2Bx sticks since his 2001 return to Slayer.
