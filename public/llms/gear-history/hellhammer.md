## Gear Timeline

### De Mysteriis Dom Sathanas Era (1988–1994)

- **Drums:** Sonor Kit (developing endorsement) — 5-piece; 22"x16" kick, 10"x9"/12"x10" racks, 16"x16" floor
- **Snare:** Sonor steel snare 14"x5.5", tuned bright
- **Cymbals:** Paiste (developing setup) — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crash, 20" ride
- **Pedal:** Early-career double bass pedal (pre-endorsement era, chain-drive)
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B
- **Heads:** Remo Ambassador
- **Original setup cost (1994):** ~$2,068
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$4,571
- **Notable:** Hellhammer joined Mayhem in 1988, replacing founding drummer Manheim. De Mysteriis Dom Sathanas (1994), recorded at Grieghallen Studio in Bergen under producer Pytten, is the benchmark black metal album and drum performance — raw, fast, and precise on a modest, developing-endorsement Sonor kit. His snare was tuned bright to cut through the album's cold, layered guitar mix.

### Dimmu Borgir / Mayhem Dual Era (1999–2013)

- **Drums:** Sonor SQ2 Heavy Beech — flagship beech shells
- **Snare:** Sonor SQ2 Heavy Beech Snare 14"x6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Paiste RUDE Series (full setup) — hi-hats, crashes, ride
- **Pedal:** Axis Double Pedal (direct-drive)
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth 5B
- **Heads:** Remo Ambassador / Pinstripe
- **Estimated setup cost (1999):** ~$5,500
- **Notable:** In 1999, Hellhammer took on a second, simultaneous career, joining symphonic black metal act Dimmu Borgir while remaining Mayhem's drummer — a dual commitment unique in black metal. He upgraded to Sonor's flagship SQ2 Heavy Beech line and switched to the Axis pedal's direct-drive mechanism for zero mechanical delay at extreme blast-beat tempos.

### Ordo Ad Chao Era (2007)

- **Drums:** Sonor SQ2 Heavy Beech (continued)
- **Snare:** Sonor SQ2 Heavy Beech Snare 14"x6.5" (continued)
- **Cymbals:** Paiste RUDE Series (continued)
- **Pedal:** Axis Double Pedal
- **Estimated setup cost (2007):** ~$8,000
- **Notable:** Mayhem's Ordo Ad Chao (2007), recorded without a bassist, is among Hellhammer's most demanding studio performances — the drums and guitars alone carry the structural weight of the record.

### Daemon Era (2014–present)

- **Drums:** Sonor SQ2 Heavy Beech (confirmed current configuration) — flagship beech shells, dual 22" kick configuration
- **Snare:** Sonor SQ2 Heavy Beech Snare 14"x6.5" (continued)
- **Cymbals:** Paiste RUDE hi-hats (14"), RUDE crashes (18", 19"), RUDE ride (20"), RUDE china (18")
- **Pedal:** Axis Double Pedal, played barefoot
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B (unchanged since his earliest Mayhem years)
- **Heads:** Remo Ambassador / Pinstripe
- **Estimated setup cost (2019):** ~$9,200
- **Notable:** Esoteric Warfare (2014) and Daemon (2019) balance Mayhem's black metal identity with unexpected melodic range. Hellhammer's full Paiste RUDE cymbal setup remains confirmed as his current configuration, and he remains famous for playing barefoot, citing direct tactile feedback through the pedal as essential to his tempo accuracy above 200 BPM.

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit does Hellhammer play?**  
A: Hellhammer plays a Sonor SQ2 Heavy Beech kit — flagship beech shells he upgraded to in 1999 when he joined Dimmu Borgir while remaining Mayhem's drummer. He started on a developing-endorsement Sonor kit in the late 1980s before settling on the SQ2 Heavy Beech as his confirmed current configuration.

**Q: What drums did Hellhammer use on De Mysteriis Dom Sathanas?**  
A: On De Mysteriis Dom Sathanas (1994), Hellhammer used a modest, developing-endorsement Sonor kit with Paiste cymbals and a chain-drive double pedal. The complete setup cost approximately $2,068, equivalent to about $4,571 adjusted for 2026 inflation.

**Q: Why does Hellhammer play barefoot?**  
A: Hellhammer plays without shoes because it gives him direct tactile feedback through the bass drum pedal — feedback that shoe soles absorb and distort. At blast-beat tempos exceeding 200 BPM, that physical connection is essential to maintaining tempo accuracy.

**Q: Is Hellhammer still in Dimmu Borgir?**  
A: Yes. Hellhammer joined Dimmu Borgir in 1999 while remaining Mayhem's drummer, maintaining both roles simultaneously ever since — one of the only drummers in extreme metal to sustain dual membership in two major bands for over two decades.

**Q: What cymbals and pedals does Hellhammer use?**  
A: Hellhammer runs a Paiste RUDE setup — hi-hats, crashes, ride, and china — driven by an Axis Double Pedal, whose direct-drive mechanism eliminates mechanical delay for precise blast-beat execution.
