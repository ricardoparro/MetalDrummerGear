## Gear Timeline

### The War Within Era (2004–2012, Shadows Fall)

- **Drums:** Mapex Saturn V — all-maple shell pack; double 22"x18" bass drums, 10"/12" rack toms, 14"/16" floor toms
- **Snare:** Mapex steel 14"x6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian K & A Custom Series — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride, K China
- **Pedals:** Mapex Falcon double pedal
- **Sticks:** ProMark 5BX
- **Heads:** Evans G2 Coated
- **Original setup cost (2004):** ~$5,500
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$8,200
- **Notable:** Bittner joined Shadows Fall in 2001 already endorsing Mapex drums and Zildjian cymbals, both signed in 1997, with a Mapex Falcon double pedal added in 2002. The Mapex Saturn V — his defining kit — carried him through The Art of Balance (2002), The War Within (2004) — the band's Grammy-nominated NWOAHM breakthrough — Threads of Life (2007), and Fire from the Sky (2012).

### Overkill / Category 7 Era (2017–present)

- **Drums:** Mapex Saturn V — all-maple shell pack, unchanged from the Shadows Fall years
- **Snare:** Mapex Black Panther 14"x6.5" Brass
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian K & A Custom Series — 14" K hi-hats, 18"/19" A Custom crashes, 21" K Custom ride, 18" K China
- **Pedals:** Mapex Falcon double pedal
- **Sticks:** Promark Jason Bittner Signature 5BX
- **Heads:** Evans G2 Coated / EMAD (kick)
- **Notable:** After Shadows Fall's 2015 breakup and a 2013–2017 stretch with Flotsam and Jetsam (plus session work on Sanctuary's 2014 comeback The Year the Sun Died), Bittner joined Overkill in 2017, replacing Ron Lipnicki, carrying his long-standing Mapex/Zildjian rig unchanged into the new chair. That same rig has carried through his work in thrash supergroup Category 7 since 2024.

## Key Gear Changes

- **1997:** Signs with Mapex and Zildjian, years before joining Shadows Fall
- **2001:** Joined Shadows Fall, bringing his existing Mapex/Zildjian endorsements
- **2002:** Mapex Falcon double pedal endorsement begins, in place for The Art of Balance
- **2004:** The War Within recorded — Shadows Fall's Grammy-nominated commercial breakthrough
- **2015:** Shadows Fall disbands; Bittner carries the same Mapex/Zildjian rig into Flotsam and Jetsam
- **2017:** Joins Overkill, replacing Ron Lipnicki — carries the same Mapex/Zildjian rig into the new chair, no brand change
- **2024:** Same Mapex/Zildjian rig carries into thrash supergroup Category 7
- **Career-long constant:** A working touring drummer's approach — durability and consistency across grueling road schedules over rare or exotic materials, and a single unbroken Mapex/Zildjian endorsement since 1997

## FAQ

**Q: What drums did Jason Bittner use on The War Within?**  
A: Jason Bittner played a Mapex Saturn V all-maple kit on The War Within (2004), with a Mapex steel 14"x6.5" snare and Zildjian K & A Custom cymbals — part of the Mapex and Zildjian endorsements he'd held since 1997. The complete setup cost approximately $5,500 in 2004 dollars — equivalent to about $8,200 adjusted for 2026 inflation.

**Q: What drum kit does Jason Bittner play now?**  
A: Jason Bittner currently plays a Mapex Saturn V all-maple kit with Zildjian K & A Custom cymbals and a Mapex Black Panther 14"x6.5" Brass snare — the same Mapex/Zildjian rig he's endorsed since 1997, carried unchanged through his Shadows Fall years into Overkill since 2017 and the thrash supergroup Category 7 since 2024.

**Q: How much would Jason Bittner's Shadows Fall-era drum kit cost today?**  
A: The original 2004 Mapex Saturn V setup cost approximately $5,500. Adjusted for inflation to 2026 dollars, that's roughly $8,200 — reflecting the working touring drummer's rig Bittner used across Shadows Fall's The Art of Balance (2002), The War Within (2004), Threads of Life (2007), and Fire from the Sky (2012).

**Q: Did Jason Bittner ever switch drum brands?**  
A: No — Bittner has played a continuous Mapex/Zildjian rig since signing with both brands in 1997, before he even joined Shadows Fall. The same setup carried through his Shadows Fall tenure, a 2013–2017 stretch with Flotsam and Jetsam, and into Overkill after he joined in 2017, with no brand change at any point.
