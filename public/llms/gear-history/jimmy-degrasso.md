## Gear Timeline

### The World Needs a Hero Era (2001)

- **Drums:** Pearl Masters Maple Complete — professional maple shell pack (representative model); 22" bass drum, 10"/12" rack toms, 16" floor tom
- **Snare:** Pearl Free-Floating Steel 14"x6.5" — free-floating lugs
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AA Series — 14" AA hi-hats, 16"/18" AA crash, 20" AA ride, 18" AA china
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark 747 Oak — oak, wood tip
- **Heads:** Evans Genera / G2 — coated batter, clear tom heads
- **Original setup cost (2001):** ~$4,671
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$8,645
- **Notable:** His Pearl/Sabian/Promark/Evans core, all confirmed brand endorsements, was assembled after joining Megadeth in 1998 with only days of notice to learn the band's full back catalogue. Pearl's flagship Masters Maple line is used as the representative professional-tier model since DeGrasso's exact Megadeth-era shell configuration isn't independently itemized.

### JD1455 Signature Snare Era (2008–present)

- **Snare:** Pearl JD1455 Jimmy DeGrasso Signature — 14"x5.5" hammered brass shell, gold-plated hardware, unveiled at the 2008 NAMM Show
- **Notable:** Seven years after The World Needs a Hero, Pearl honored DeGrasso with a dedicated signature snare — making him one of relatively few touring metal drummers with a namesake production snare.

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Jimmy DeGrasso use in Megadeth?**
A: Jimmy DeGrasso used a Pearl Masters Maple-tier kit (22" bass drum, 10"/12" rack toms, 16" floor tom) with a Pearl Free-Floating Steel 14"x6.5" snare, Sabian AA cymbals, Pro-Mark 747 Oak sticks, and Evans Genera/G2 heads on 2001's The World Needs a Hero.

**Q: How much did Jimmy DeGrasso's Megadeth-era drum kit cost?**
A: DeGrasso's Pearl/Sabian/Promark/Evans rig cost approximately $4,671 in 2001 dollars, equivalent to roughly $8,645 today after inflation.

**Q: What is the Pearl JD1455 snare?**
A: The Pearl JD1455 is Jimmy DeGrasso's own signature snare — a 14"x5.5" hammered brass shell with gold-plated hardware — unveiled at the 2008 NAMM Show, seven years after The World Needs a Hero and six years after his Megadeth tenure ended.

**Q: What cymbals does Jimmy DeGrasso use?**
A: Jimmy DeGrasso plays Sabian AA Series cymbals, confirmed via Sabian's own artist page — a relationship established during his 1998–2002 Megadeth tenure and maintained since.

**Q: Did Jimmy DeGrasso always play Pearl drums?**
A: DeGrasso's Pearl endorsement is confirmed via his own gear record and Sabian's artist materials starting with his 1998 Megadeth tenure; no fixed drum brand is documented for his earlier years with Y&T, Suicidal Tendencies, or Alice Cooper.
