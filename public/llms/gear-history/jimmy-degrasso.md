## Gear Timeline

### Joins Megadeth (1998)

- **Setup cost reference:** ~$3,500
- **Notable:** DeGrasso joined Megadeth in 1998 with only days of notice to learn the band's full back catalogue, having previously toured with Y&T, Suicidal Tendencies, and Alice Cooper.

### The World Needs a Hero Era (2001)

- **Drums:** Pearl Masters Maple Complete — professional maple shell pack (representative model); 22" bass drum, 10"/12" rack toms, 16" floor tom
- **Snare:** Pearl Free-Floating Steel 14"x6.5" shell, free-floating lugs
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AA Series — 14" AA hi-hats, 16"/18" AA crash, 20" AA ride, 18" AA china
- **Sticks:** Pro-Mark 747 Oak Rock model, wood tip
- **Heads:** Evans Genera G2 tom batters, coated snare batter
- **Original setup cost (2001):** ~$4,671
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$8,645
- **Notable:** Pearl endorsement confirmed via DeGrasso's own gear record and Sabian's artist materials; his exact Megadeth-era shell configuration isn't independently itemized, so Pearl's flagship Masters Maple line is used as the representative professional-tier model. His Pearl Free-Floating snare was the brand's flagship snare technology through the era — his own JD1455 signature model was still seven years away.

### JD1455 Signature Snare (2008)

- **Snare:** Pearl JD1455 Jimmy DeGrasso Signature 14"x5.5" hammered brass shell, gold-plated hardware
- **Setup cost reference:** ~$5,200
- **Notable:** Pearl unveiled a dedicated JD1455 signature snare at NAMM, years after his Megadeth tenure ended — making DeGrasso one of relatively few touring metal drummers with a namesake production snare.

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Jimmy DeGrasso use in Megadeth?**
A: Jimmy DeGrasso's 2001 Megadeth-era rig, recorded for "The World Needs a Hero," centered on a Pearl Masters Maple shell pack (used here as the representative model, since his exact shell configuration isn't independently itemized), a Pearl Free-Floating Steel snare, Sabian AA cymbals, Pro-Mark 747 Oak sticks, and Evans Genera G2 heads.

**Q: How much did Jimmy DeGrasso's drum setup cost?**
A: DeGrasso's documented 2001 Megadeth-era setup cost approximately $4,671 at the time, equivalent to roughly $8,645 today after inflation.

**Q: What is the Pearl JD1455 snare?**
A: The Pearl JD1455 is Jimmy DeGrasso's signature snare, a 14"x5.5" hammered brass shell with gold-plated hardware, unveiled at NAMM 2008 — six years after his Megadeth tenure ended, so it postdates the band's studio albums rather than being used on them.

**Q: What cymbals and sticks does Jimmy DeGrasso use?**
A: DeGrasso has used Sabian AA Series cymbals and Pro-Mark drumsticks since his Megadeth tenure began in 1998, endorsements confirmed via Sabian's own artist page and his own gear record.
