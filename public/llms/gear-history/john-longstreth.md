## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did John Longstreth use on Chaosmos?**
A: John Longstreth used a Pearl Masters Maple-tier kit (22" bass drum, 10"/12" rack toms, 16" floor tom) with Meinl Classics Custom Dark cymbals, ACD Unlimited bass drum pedals with FootBlaster triggers, and his own Scorpion Percussion "Wee Heavy" signature sticks on Origin's 2022 album Chaosmos.

**Q: How much does John Longstreth's drum setup cost?**
A: Longstreth's Origin-era rig cost approximately $4,273 at 2022 retail, equivalent to roughly $4,784 today after inflation.

**Q: What cymbals does John Longstreth use?**
A: Per Meinl's own artist page, Longstreth uses the Classics Custom Dark series — an 8" Dark Splash, 14" Dark Hi-Hats, and 18" Dark China — plus a 12"/14" Generation X Trash Hat for the metallic choked accents in his Origin patterns.

**Q: Does John Longstreth have signature drumsticks?**
A: Yes — John Longstreth's "Wee Heavy" signature drumstick model is made with Scorpion Percussion, referenced directly on his own Scorpion Percussion artist page.

**Q: What pedals does John Longstreth use?**
A: John Longstreth uses ACD Unlimited bass drum pedals paired with FootBlaster triggers, a boutique Austrian pedal and trigger system built for sustained high-speed blast-beat endurance.
