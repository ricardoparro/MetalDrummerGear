## FAQ

**Q: How much does Martin Lopez's drum kit cost?**
A: Martin Lopez's 1998 My Arms, Your Hearse-era Sonor Designer Series setup cost approximately $5,143. Adjusted for 2026 inflation, that's equivalent to roughly $10,336.

**Q: What drums did Martin Lopez use on My Arms, Your Hearse?**
A: Lopez used a Sonor Designer Series maple kit — the same setup he played through Opeth's "Blackwater Park" (2001), "Deliverance" (2002), and "Ghost Reveries" (2005) — chosen for a tone that let his jazz-inflected dynamics breathe against Opeth's progressive death metal arrangements. Specific shell sizes are not independently verified.

**Q: What cymbals did Martin Lopez use with Opeth?**
A: Lopez used Sabian HH and AAX cymbals throughout his Opeth tenure (1997–2006), chosen for warmth and dynamic complexity over brighter, more aggressive metal cymbals. Specific cymbal sizes are not independently verified.

**Q: Does Martin Lopez still use the same gear today?**
A: No — Lopez played Sonor Designer Series drums and Sabian HH/AAX cymbals throughout his Opeth years (1997–2006), then switched to a Noble & Cooley Walnut shell pack and Zildjian A/K series cymbals after co-founding Soen in 2010, a configuration he has used ever since.
