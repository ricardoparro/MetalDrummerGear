## Gear Timeline

### Self-Titled Debut Era (2009–2011)

- **Drums:** Mapex — entry-level shell pack, pre-endorsement
- **Snare:** Mapex standard steel snare
- **Cymbals:** Istanbul Agop — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride
- **Pedal:** Mapex double pedal
- **Sticks:** Promark, standard hickory (pre-signature)
- **Heads:** Evans, standard clear/coated
- **Notable:** Released April 20, 2010 on Sumerian Records, Periphery's self-titled debut codified djent as a genre. Halpern's gear in this window was modest and pre-endorsement — Mapex drums and snare, Istanbul Agop cymbals — but the playing itself set a new standard for precision in progressive metal.

### Periphery II Transitional Era (2012–2014)

- **Drums:** Yamaha — standard shell pack, signed endorsement
- **Snare:** Yamaha standard steel snare
- **Cymbals:** Meinl Byzance Series — switched from Istanbul Agop
- **Pedal:** Yamaha double pedal
- **Sticks:** Promark, hickory (slightly heavier for live power)
- **Heads:** Evans, standard heads across toms and kick
- **Notable:** "Periphery II: This Time It's Personal" (2012) and the "Clear" EP (2014) ran on Halpern's Yamaha endorsement. This era also marked his switch from Istanbul Agop to Meinl Byzance cymbals, the dark Turkish B20 voice that has defined his sound ever since.

### Select Difficulty Era (2015–2018) — Grammy-Nominated

- **Drums:** Pearl Reference Series — maple/birch hybrid shells, 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Pearl Matt Halpern Signature Snare (MH1460/B), 14"×6" brass shell
- **Cymbals:** Meinl Byzance Brilliant / Dark — 14" Byzance Brilliant hi-hats, 16"/18" Byzance Dark crashes, 21" Byzance Brilliant ride
- **Pedal:** Pearl Demon Drive Double Pedal
- **Sticks:** Promark, standard hickory
- **Heads:** Evans, standard heads across toms and kick
- **Original setup cost (2015–2018):** ~$12,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$16,000
- **Notable:** "Periphery III: Select Difficulty" earned a Grammy nomination for Best Metal Performance for "The Price Is Wrong" at the 59th Grammy Awards — the first Grammy nomination for any djent-identified act. Halpern signed with Pearl in 2015, establishing the Pearl Reference Series kit and brass signature snare that remain his cornerstone setup.

### Hail Stan / Aliens Era (2019–Present)

- **Drums:** Pearl Reference Series — maple/birch hybrid shells, custom finish, 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Pearl Matt Halpern Signature Snare (MH1460/B), 14"×6" brass shell (continued)
- **Cymbals:** Meinl Byzance Extra Dry / Dark — 14" Byzance Extra Dry hi-hats, 16"/18" Byzance Dark crashes, 21" Byzance Brilliant ride, plus his signature Meinl Artist Concept "Double Down Stack"
- **Pedal:** Pearl Demon Drive Double Pedal, Pearl D-2000 Throne
- **Sticks:** ProMark TXMHW signature model (2B weight, plays like 5B)
- **Heads:** Evans
- **Original setup cost (2019–present):** ~$18,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$18,000
- **Notable:** "Periphery IV: Hail Stan" (2019) and "Periphery V: Djent Is Not a Genre" (2023) showcase Halpern's fully mature Pearl Reference Series configuration, now paired with his ProMark TXMHW signature sticks and a Roland SPD-SX sampling pad for live electronics — a flagship-tier setup reflecting over a decade of continuous Pearl endorsement.

## Key Gear Changes

- **2009–2011:** Pre-endorsement Mapex drums and snare, Istanbul Agop cymbals on the genre-defining self-titled debut
- **2012:** Yamaha drum endorsement begins; switch from Istanbul Agop to Meinl Byzance cymbals on "Periphery II"
- **2015:** Pearl endorsement begins, succeeding Yamaha — Pearl Reference Series kit and Pearl Matt Halpern Signature Snare (MH1460/B) established, Grammy-nominated "Select Difficulty" era
- **2019–present:** Meinl Byzance Extra Dry hi-hats adopted; ProMark TXMHW signature sticks launched for "Hail Stan" and "Djent Is Not a Genre"

## FAQ

**Q: What gear does Matt Halpern use?**  
A: Matt Halpern currently plays a Pearl Reference Series drum kit with his Pearl Matt Halpern Signature Snare (MH1460/B), a comprehensive Meinl Byzance cymbal setup, a Pearl Demon Drive double pedal, and ProMark TXMHW signature sticks. His gear evolved from a pre-endorsement Mapex setup with Istanbul Agop cymbals on Periphery's 2010 debut, through a Yamaha endorsement from 2012–2014, to the Pearl partnership he has held continuously since 2015.

**Q: What is Matt Halpern's signature snare?**  
A: Matt Halpern's signature snare since 2015 has been the Pearl Matt Halpern Signature Snare (MH1460/B) — a 14"×6" brass shell snare. It succeeded a stock Mapex snare used on Periphery's debut and a Yamaha snare during the 2012–2014 era.

**Q: How much does Matt Halpern's drum kit cost?**  
A: Matt Halpern's 2015–2018 "Select Difficulty" Pearl Reference setup cost approximately $12,000, equivalent to roughly $16,000 in 2026 after inflation. His current Hail Stan/Aliens-era Pearl Reference setup runs closer to $18,000 including the signature snare, full Meinl Byzance cymbal arsenal, and electronics.

**Q: Was Periphery III: Select Difficulty nominated for a Grammy?**  
A: Yes. "Periphery III: Select Difficulty" (2016) earned a Grammy nomination for Best Metal Performance at the 59th Grammy Awards in 2017 for the track "The Price Is Wrong" — the first Grammy nomination for any djent-identified act.

**Q: What cymbals does Matt Halpern use?**  
A: Matt Halpern uses Meinl Byzance cymbals spanning the Extra Dry, Dark, and Brilliant lines, including 14" Byzance Extra Dry hi-hats, 16"/18" Byzance Dark crashes, a 21" Byzance Brilliant ride, and his signature Meinl Artist Concept "Double Down Stack". The handcrafted B20 bronze Byzance line has been his cymbal voice since the Periphery II transitional era around 2012, succeeding the Istanbul Agop cymbals he played on the band's debut.
