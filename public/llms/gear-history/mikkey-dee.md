## Gear Timeline

### King Diamond Era (1985–1990)

- **Drums:** Various kits (no single fixed brand documented) — birch shells, 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Stock birch snare 14"x5.5"
- **Cymbals:** Paiste Signature Series — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride
- **Pedal:** Standard cam-driven double pedal
- **Sticks:** Wincent 5B
- **Heads:** Remo Ambassador
- **Estimated cost (1985–1990):** ~$5,000
- **Notable:** Dee joined King Diamond's band in 1985, recording Fatal Portrait (1986) and Abigail (1987) before a brief stint with Don Dokken's band in 1988, then returning as a session player on Conspiracy (1989) and The Eye (1990). The King Diamond years were his professional apprenticeship. His Paiste cymbal endorsement, established in 1987, was the one constant that carried through everything that followed.

### Early Motörhead Era (1993–2004)

- **Drums:** Tama Artstar II / Swingstar — birch/maple shell pack, 22"x18" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Tama steel snare 14"x6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Paiste Signature Series — 14" hi-hats, 16"/18" crashes, 20" ride
- **Pedal:** DW 5000 Double Pedal
- **Sticks:** Wincent 5B
- **Heads:** Remo Emperor (toms), Powerstroke 3 (kick)
- **Original setup cost (1993):** ~$7,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$13,000
- **Notable:** Dee joined Motörhead in 1992, replacing Phil "Philthy Animal" Taylor ahead of Bastards (1993) — his studio debut with the band. He joined on a Tama kit; his Paiste cymbals, already in place since 1987, carried over unchanged.

### Late Motörhead / Bad Magic Era (2004–2015)

- **Drums:** Tama Artstar II / Swingstar (2004–2012), then Sonor SQ2 — maple/birch hybrid, 22"x18" kick, 8"/10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Sonor SQ2 Mikkey Dee Signature Snare, 14"x7.25" birch
- **Cymbals:** Paiste Signature Series (expanded configuration) — 14" hi-hats, 17"/18" crashes, 21" ride, 19" china
- **Pedal:** DW 5000 Series Double Pedal
- **Sticks:** Wincent Mikkey Dee Signature
- **Heads:** Evans G2 (toms), EMAD (kick)
- **Estimated cost (2015):** ~$18,000
- **Notable:** Kiss of Death (2006) through Bad Magic (2015) documented Motörhead's final era. Dee signed with Sonor in 2012, replacing Tama as his primary kit and releasing a Mikkey Dee Signature Snare as part of the deal. Bad Magic won a Grammy for Best Metal Performance ("Thunder & Lightning") — Motörhead's only Grammy, awarded weeks before Lemmy Kilmister's death on December 28, 2015.

### Scorpions Era (2016–present)

- **Drums:** Sonor SQ2 (continued)
- **Snare:** Sonor SQ2 Mikkey Dee Signature Snare, 14"x7.25" birch
- **Cymbals:** Paiste Signature Series (continued)
- **Pedal:** DW 5000 Series Double Pedal
- **Sticks:** Wincent Mikkey Dee Signature
- **Heads:** Evans G2 / EMAD2
- **Estimated cost (current):** ~$22,000
- **Notable:** Following Lemmy's death and Motörhead's dissolution, Dee joined Scorpions in 2016, carrying over the Sonor SQ2/Paiste/Evans configuration developed in Motörhead's final years, adapted for Scorpions' more melodic hard rock context.

## Key Gear Changes

- **1987:** Signs with Paiste for cymbals — the one endorsement that carries unbroken through King Diamond, Motörhead, and Scorpions
- **1993:** Joins Motörhead on Bastards, playing a Tama Artstar II/Swingstar kit with Paiste cymbals carried over
- **2012:** Signs with Sonor, switching from Tama to a Sonor SQ2 kit with a Mikkey Dee Signature Snare for the final Motörhead era
- **2015:** Sonor SQ2, DW 5000 Series double pedal, and Wincent Mikkey Dee signature sticks close out Bad Magic — Motörhead's final album and only Grammy win
- **2016:** Joins Scorpions, continuing the Sonor SQ2/Paiste/Evans configuration
- **Career constant:** Paiste cymbals, unbroken since 1987, through King Diamond, Motörhead, and Scorpions

## FAQ

**Q: How much does Mikkey Dee's drum kit cost?**
A: Mikkey Dee's 1993 Bastards-era Tama Artstar II/Paiste setup cost approximately $7,000. Adjusted for 2026 inflation, that's equivalent to roughly $13,000. His late-Motörhead Sonor SQ2/Paiste setup, refined through Bad Magic (2015), is valued at approximately $18,000–$22,000 at current retail.

**Q: What drums does Mikkey Dee play?**
A: Mikkey Dee played Tama Artstar II/Swingstar drums for his Motörhead studio debut on Bastards (1993) through 2012, when he signed with Sonor and switched to a Sonor SQ2 kit with a Mikkey Dee Signature Snare — the kit he used through Motörhead's final album, Bad Magic (2015), and continues to use with Scorpions.

**Q: What cymbals does Mikkey Dee use?**
A: Dee has played Paiste Signature Series cymbals since 1987 — an endorsement unbroken through King Diamond, his entire Motörhead tenure, and his current Scorpions run, with an expanded configuration added in the later Motörhead years.

**Q: How long was Mikkey Dee Motörhead's drummer?**
A: Mikkey Dee was Motörhead's drummer for 23 years, from Bastards (1993) until Lemmy Kilmister's death on December 28, 2015, following the band's final album, Bad Magic. He joined Scorpions in 2016.

**Q: Has Mikkey Dee always played the same drum kit?**
A: No — he had no fixed drum brand during his 1985–1990 King Diamond years, switched to Tama Artstar II/Swingstar for his 1993 Motörhead debut on Bastards, and signed with Sonor in 2012 for a Sonor SQ2 kit, a setup he has carried into his current Scorpions tenure. His Paiste cymbal endorsement, by contrast, has been unbroken since 1987.
