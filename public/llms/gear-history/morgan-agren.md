## FAQ

**Q: How much does Morgan Ågren's drum kit cost?**
A: Morgan Ågren's current Sonor SQ2 Beech setup, established in 2015, cost approximately $25,000 and remains roughly $25,000 in 2026 dollars.

**Q: What drums does Morgan Ågren play?**
A: Ågren plays a Sonor SQ2 Beech kit — beech shells with a 22" kick and 8"/10"/12"/14"/16" toms, paired with a Sonor SQ2 Beech 14"x5" snare plus a secondary piccolo and a Sonor Giant Step double pedal. He has played Sonor since signing to the brand's SQ2 custom program in 2012, around the time Devin Townsend recruited him for Epicloud; the drum brand he used earlier in his career is not publicly documented.

**Q: What cymbals does Morgan Ågren use?**
A: Ågren plays a multi-series Paiste arrangement combining Signature and 2002 Series pieces, a relationship dating back to 1988. His Paiste cymbals are prized for their controlled, balanced tone suited to improvisational and avant-garde contexts.

**Q: Did Morgan Ågren work with Frank Zappa?**
A: Yes, but not as a touring member — Morgan Ågren was personally hand-picked by Frank Zappa to sit in with his band for one Stockholm concert in 1988, at age 20, and Zappa reportedly called him one of the greatest drummers he had ever encountered. Zappa's subsequent illness (diagnosed with prostate cancer in 1990) limited any further collaboration, but the 1988 guest appearance had already brought Ågren international recognition. Zappa died in December 1993.

**Q: What makes Morgan Ågren's drumming unique?**
A: Ågren's playing combines extraordinary technical command with deep musical intelligence. His polyrhythmic vocabulary, metric modulation, and ghost note sensitivity operate at a level rarely encountered even among the world's most accomplished drummers. He plays with a light touch (5A sticks, open Ambassador/Emperor Coated heads) for extraordinary dynamic range.

**Q: Is Morgan Ågren still in Devin Townsend Project?**
A: No — Ågren's Devin Townsend Project tenure was a closed four-year run from 2012 to 2016, ending with Transcendence (2016). He remains active with the Mats/Morgan Band and continues to tour and record on his Sonor SQ2 Beech / Paiste setup.
