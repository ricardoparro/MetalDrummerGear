## Gear Timeline

### Joins Dimmu Borgir (1999)

- **Setup cost reference:** ~$3,000
- **Notable:** Barker departs Cradle of Filth and joins Norwegian symphonic black metal band Dimmu Borgir.

### Death Cult Armageddon Era (2003)

- **Drums:** Pearl Masterworks Custom — 10-ply maple, special-order all-black hardware; 20"x18" bass drums (x2), 10"/12"/13"/14" rack toms, 16" floor tom
- **Snare:** Pearl Masterworks FM-1435C 14"x3.5" free-floating piccolo, piano black maple shell
- **Cymbals:** Paiste RUDE / Dimensions (special-order black) — 24" Dimensions Mega Power Bell Ride, 22" Power Ride, 21" RUDE China, assorted crashes/hi-hats/splash
- **Hardware:** Pearl Icon Hardware (special-order black) — Mastercast hoops, Optimount, Icon curved rack
- **Original setup cost (2003):** ~$8,570
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$15,259
- **Notable:** This setup is documented in unusual detail thanks to a 2009 Sick Drummer Magazine listing selling Barker's original kit — a special-order, handmade-to-order Pearl Masterworks 10-ply maple shell pack with all-black hardware (ordered in 2003), paired with special-order black Paiste RUDE and Dimensions cymbals, including a rare, later-discontinued 21" RUDE China cut. Barker is also a longtime official Sonor artist, credited by Sonor for his Cradle of Filth and Dimmu Borgir tenures, though Sonor's own artist materials don't itemize a specific kit spec for his current setup.

### Kit Listed for Sale (2009)

- **Setup cost reference:** ~$9,200
- **Notable:** Barker's original Death Cult Armageddon-era Pearl Masterworks kit is documented and sold via eBay / Sick Drummer Magazine, corroborating the exact component list above.

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Nick Barker use on Death Cult Armageddon?**
A: Nick Barker used a special-order Pearl Masterworks Custom kit — 10-ply maple shells with all-black hardware, dual 20"x18" bass drums, and 10"/12"/13"/14" rack toms with a 16" floor tom — paired with a Pearl Masterworks FM-1435C 14"x3.5" piccolo snare and special-order black Paiste RUDE/Dimensions cymbals, per a 2009 Sick Drummer Magazine listing selling the original kit.

**Q: How much did Nick Barker's drum setup cost?**
A: Barker's documented 2003 Death Cult Armageddon-era Pearl Masterworks rig cost approximately $8,570 at the time, equivalent to roughly $15,259 today after inflation.

**Q: What cymbals did Nick Barker use with Dimmu Borgir?**
A: Barker used special-order black Paiste RUDE and Dimensions cymbals, including a 24" Dimensions Mega Power Bell Ride, a 22" Power Ride, and a rare, later-discontinued 21" RUDE China cut, itemized in the 2009 kit-for-sale listing.

**Q: Is Nick Barker a Sonor artist?**
A: Yes — Nick Barker is a longtime official Sonor artist, credited by Sonor's own artist page for his Cradle of Filth and Dimmu Borgir tenures. Sonor's own materials don't publish a component-level spec sheet for his kit, which is why the itemized Death Cult Armageddon-era pricing above comes from the independently documented Pearl Masterworks kit instead.
