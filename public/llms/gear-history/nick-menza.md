## Gear Timeline

### Rust in Peace Era (1990–1992)

- **Drums:** Tama Artstar II — 5-piece, Midnight Blue finish; 22"x16" kick, 12"x10" and 13"x11" racks, 16"x16" floor
- **Snare:** Tama Steel 14"x5.5"
- **Cymbals:** Zildjian A Series — 14" hi-hats, 16" crash, 18" crash, 20" ride
- **Pedal:** DW 5000 double pedal
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth 5B Hickory
- **Heads:** Remo Ambassador (toms), Powerstroke P3 (kick)
- **Original setup cost (1990):** ~$1,898
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$4,757
- **Notable:** Menza's birch-shell Tama Artstar II — a pro-tier kit, not a budget setup — carried through Rust in Peace (1990) with no gear change from his 1989 arrival in Megadeth through the album's 1990–1992 touring cycle. The tight, dry Rust in Peace drum tone cut through Dave Mustaine and Marty Friedman's dense guitar arrangements on "Holy Wars," "Hangar 18," and "Tornado of Souls." He switched to Pearl drums only at the start of the Countdown to Extinction cycle in 1992 — not during Rust in Peace itself.

### Countdown to Extinction / Youthanasia Era (1992–1996)

- **Drums:** Pearl Masters Professional, upgrading to Pearl Masterworks for Youthanasia — maple shells, custom finish; 22" kick, 10"/12"/14"/16" toms
- **Snare:** Pearl Free-Floating Brass 14"x6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Paiste 2002 / Signature Series — switched from Zildjian for Countdown to Extinction's more melodic, arena-rock-oriented production
- **Pedal:** Pearl Eliminator double pedal
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth 2B
- **Heads:** Remo Emperor (toms), EMAD (kick)
- **Original setup cost (1992):** ~$12,000
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$25,000
- **Notable:** Menza switched from Tama to Pearl for Countdown to Extinction (1992), Megadeth's commercial breakthrough, then upgraded to the premium Pearl Masterworks custom tier for Youthanasia (1994) as the band moved into arena headlining status. The brass Free-Floating snare and Paiste cymbal switch gave a warmer, more polished tone suited to the band's more accessible songwriting, without losing the precision that defined Rust in Peace.

### Cryptic Writings Era (1996–1998)

- **Drums:** Pearl Reference Custom — Pearl's flagship tier; specific shell configuration not independently documented
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AA / Signature Series — specific size configuration not independently documented
- **Notable:** Menza's final full studio album with Megadeth, Cryptic Writings (1997), saw him upgrade to the flagship Pearl Reference Custom kit — moving on from the Pearl Masterworks he'd played since Youthanasia — and switch to Sabian AA / Signature Series cymbals; no Zildjian or Paiste cymbals are documented for this era. He was fired from Megadeth in 1998 following a knee injury.

### Post-Megadeth / OHM Era (2004–2016)

- **Notable:** No independently verified gear information exists for Menza's post-Megadeth years, including his brief 2004 Megadeth reunion dates and his work with jazz-metal fusion group OHM. He passed away on May 21, 2016, collapsing on stage during an OHM performance in Woodland Hills, California.

## Key Gear Changes

- **1989–1992:** Tama Artstar II established at Megadeth arrival (1989) and carried through Rust in Peace (1990) with no gear change
- **1992:** Switched from Tama to Pearl Masters Professional, and from Zildjian to Paiste 2002 / Signature cymbals, for Countdown to Extinction — Megadeth's commercial breakthrough
- **1994:** Upgraded to Pearl Masterworks for Youthanasia — the most premium kit of his Megadeth career
- **1997:** Upgraded to Pearl Reference Custom and switched to Sabian AA / Signature Series cymbals for Cryptic Writings — his final full Megadeth studio album
- **2004–2016:** No independently verified gear information exists for his post-Megadeth and OHM years

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Nick Menza use on Rust in Peace?**
A: Nick Menza played a Tama Artstar II on Rust in Peace (1990) — a pro-tier birch-shell kit in Midnight Blue finish, not a budget setup. He paired it with Zildjian A Series cymbals and a DW 5000 double pedal. The complete setup cost approximately $1,898 at the time, equivalent to about $4,757 adjusted for 2026 inflation. He played the same kit through the album's 1990–1992 touring cycle before switching to Pearl for Countdown to Extinction (1992).

**Q: What drums did Nick Menza use on Countdown to Extinction?**
A: For Countdown to Extinction (1992), Menza switched from Tama to Pearl Masters Professional, then upgraded to Pearl Masterworks for Youthanasia (1994) — the most premium kit of his Megadeth career. He also switched from Zildjian to Paiste 2002 / Signature Series cymbals and moved to a brass Pearl Free-Floating snare for a warmer tone suited to the album's more polished production.

**Q: What drums did Nick Menza use on Cryptic Writings?**
A: On Cryptic Writings (1997), his final full studio album with Megadeth, Menza played a Pearl Reference Custom — his flagship kit — paired with Sabian AA / Signature Series cymbals. No Zildjian or Paiste cymbals are documented for this era.

**Q: What cymbals did Nick Menza use?**
A: Menza used Zildjian A Series cymbals on Rust in Peace (1990), switched to Paiste 2002 / Signature Series for Countdown to Extinction (1992) and Youthanasia (1994), then switched again to Sabian AA / Signature Series for Cryptic Writings (1997). No independently verified gear information exists for his post-Megadeth years.

**Q: What gear did Nick Menza use after leaving Megadeth?**
A: No independently verified gear information exists for Nick Menza's post-Megadeth years, including his 2004 Megadeth reunion dates and his work with jazz-metal fusion group OHM. He died on May 21, 2016, collapsing on stage during an OHM performance in Woodland Hills, California.
