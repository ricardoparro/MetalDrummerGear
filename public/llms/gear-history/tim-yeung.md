## Gear Timeline

### Divine Heresy Era (2006–2010)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga — bubinga shell pack, established Divine Heresy configuration; 22"x18" bass drum (x2, double kick), 10"x8"/12"x9" rack toms, 16"x14"/18"x16" floor toms
- **Snare:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga 14"x6.5", bright tuning
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AAX Series — 14" AAX hi-hats, 16"/18" AAX crashes, 20" HHX ride, 18" AAX china
- **Pedals:** Tama Speed Cobra 910 double pedal
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth 5B
- **Heads:** Remo Powerstroke 3 (kick), Emperor Coated (toms), Coated Ambassador (snare)
- **Original setup cost (2006):** ~$3,500
- **Inflation-adjusted to 2026:** ~$4,800
- **Notable:** Yeung's recording debut came on Hate Eternal's Conquering the Throne (1999, 1997-2000 tenure); by the time he co-founded Divine Heresy with Dino Cazares and signed with Tama and Sabian for Bringer of Plagues (2009), his relentless 200–280 BPM blast beats — sustained on relaxed, rebound-based footwork rather than tension-based power striking — had made him one of technical death metal's most studied drummers.

### Current Setup (Morbid Angel era, 2011–present)

- **Drums:** Tama Starclassic Bubinga
- **Snare:** Tama S.L.P. Big Black Steel 14"x6.5"
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AAX & HHX Series — 14" AAX Stage hi-hats, 18"/19" AAX X-Plosion crashes, 21" HHX Raw Bell Dry ride
- **Pedals:** Tama Speed Cobra 910 double pedal
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth 5B
- **Heads:** Remo Powerstroke 3 / Emperor Coated
- **Notable:** Yeung joined Morbid Angel for the divisive but drum-praised Illud Divinum Insanus (2011) and returned the band to classic death metal form on Kingdoms Disdained (2017), carrying the same Tama Starclassic Bubinga/Sabian rig established in 2006 throughout.

## Key Gear Changes

- **1999:** Conquering the Throne — Yeung's recording debut with Hate Eternal (1997-2000 tenure)
- **2006:** Co-founds Divine Heresy — extreme double bass speed establishes the Tama/Sabian/Vic Firth/Remo rig
- **2009:** Bringer of Plagues — signs with Tama and Sabian, cementing the gear template carried into Morbid Angel
- **2011:** Joins Morbid Angel for Illud Divinum Insanus
- **2017:** Kingdoms Disdained — Tama Starclassic Bubinga and Speed Cobra 910 current rig established

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit does Tim Yeung use?**  
A: Tim Yeung currently plays a Tama Starclassic Bubinga kit with a Tama S.L.P. Big Black Steel snare, Sabian AAX & HHX cymbals, and a Tama Speed Cobra 910 double pedal. In his original 2006 Divine Heresy setup, he played a Tama Starclassic Bubinga kit with a matching Bubinga snare, Sabian AAX cymbals, and a Tama Speed Cobra double pedal — that original setup cost approximately $3,500, equivalent to about $4,800 adjusted for 2026 inflation.

**Q: How fast does Tim Yeung play, and what gear supports it?**  
A: Yeung is documented sustaining blast beats in the 200–280 BPM range across full sets. His gear choices consistently favor mechanical consistency over raw power — the Tama Speed Cobra double pedal on his original Divine Heresy rig and the current Tama Speed Cobra 910 both use smooth, predictable cam/rolling-glide action that rewards his relaxed, rebound-based footwork rather than tension-based striking.

**Q: How much would Tim Yeung's Divine Heresy-era drum kit cost today?**  
A: The original 2006 Tama Starclassic Bubinga setup from Yeung's Divine Heresy era cost approximately $3,500. Adjusted for inflation to 2026 dollars, that's roughly $4,800 — a professional-tier extreme metal rig rather than a boutique custom build.

**Q: Did Tim Yeung always play Sabian cymbals?**  
A: Yes — Sabian AAX cymbals have been a constant across Yeung's Divine Heresy and Morbid Angel eras, expanding to a combined AAX & HHX setup in his current rig, alongside the Tama shell relationship he's maintained since 2006.
