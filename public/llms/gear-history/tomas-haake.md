- **Heads:** Remo Coated Emperor
- **Electronics:** Roland SPD-SX trigger pad for samples and click track
- **Notable:** 2005 marked the full professionalisation of Haake's gear: a long-term Sonor drum deal, a move to Sabian HHX & AAX cymbals, and Tama Speed Cobra pedals, all still in use today. The Sonor signature snare followed in 2008 (the ÖBZen era), and the Wincent signature stick deal in 2010.

- **2005:** Signed with Sonor (SQ2 series drums), Sabian (HHX & AAX cymbals), and switched to Tama Speed Cobra single pedals — the rig he has used ever since
- **2008:** First Sonor Tomas Haake signature snare developed (ÖBZen era)
- **2010:** First Wincent Tomas Haake signature stick

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Tomas Haake use on Chaosphere?**  
A: On Chaosphere (1998), Tomas Haake was an unendorsed drummer — no drum, cymbal, or stick brand from this era is publicly documented. His first documented gear partnerships (Sonor drums, Sabian cymbals) didn't begin until 2005. Despite the unknown gear, Chaosphere is considered one of the most technically extreme drum performances on a major metal record.

**Q: What cymbals does Tomas Haake use?**  
A: Tomas Haake has used Sabian HHX & AAX Series cymbals since signing with the brand in 2005, including signature HHX Compression hi-hats, HHX Stage Crashes, a Legacy Ride, and an AAXtreme China. No cymbal brand before 2005 is publicly documented.

**Q: What drum pedal does Tomas Haake use?**  
A: Tomas Haake plays two independent Tama Speed Cobra single pedals rather than a double pedal — a setup adopted alongside his 2005 Sonor and Sabian deals. Each foot controls its own pedal, letting him play independent rhythms with each kick rather than the linked motion of a double pedal, which suits Meshuggah's precise, low-velocity double-bass patterns that prioritise accuracy over raw speed.

**Q: What sticks does Tomas Haake use?**  
A: Tomas Haake uses Wincent Tomas Haake Signature sticks, a signature model developed with Wincent since 2010. No stick brand before that deal is publicly documented.

**Q: What is Tomas Haake's current drum kit?**  
A: As of the 2020s, Tomas Haake plays a Sonor SQ2 Heavy Beech kit, a Sonor Tomas Haake Signature snare, Sabian HHX & AAX cymbals, two independent Tama Speed Cobra single pedals, Wincent Tomas Haake Signature sticks, Remo Coated Emperor heads, and a Roland SPD-SX pad for electronic integration.
