## Gear Timeline

### Cowboys from Hell / Vulgar Display of Power / Far Beyond Driven Era (1990–1996)

- **Drums:** Tama — signed with Tama during Pantera's major-label breakthrough on Cowboys from Hell
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AA / AAX Series
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B
- **Notable:** Cowboys from Hell (Atco, 1990) discarded Pantera's earlier glam image and established groove metal as a genre — Vinnie Paul's locked-in pocket drumming on tracks like "Domination," "Cemetery Gates," and "Primal Concrete Sledge" set the template that an entire decade of heavy music would follow. He continued on Tama through Vulgar Display of Power (1992) and Far Beyond Driven (1994), which debuted at #1 on the Billboard 200 — the first album this extreme-leaning to do so.

### The Great Southern Trendkill / Reinventing the Steel / Damageplan Era (1996–2008)

- **Drums:** Pearl — moved to Pearl drums, spanning The Great Southern Trendkill and Reinventing the Steel
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AA / AAX Series (unchanged)
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B (unchanged)
- **Notable:** Vinnie Paul recorded Pantera's final two studio albums, The Great Southern Trendkill (1996) and Reinventing the Steel (2000), on Pearl drums. After Pantera's 2003 breakup, he and his brother Dimebag Darrell formed Damageplan, releasing New Found Power (2004) on the same Pearl setup before Dimebag was murdered on stage on December 8, 2004, ending the band.

### Hellyeah Era (2008–2018)

- **Drums:** ddrum Vinnie Paul Signature Series — signed with ddrum, who developed his Signature Series kit and snare for Hellyeah; his final and longest-running endorsement
- **Cymbals:** Sabian AA / AAX Series (unchanged)
- **Sticks:** Vic Firth American Classic 5B (unchanged)
- **Heads:** Evans — new endorsement signed alongside the ddrum deal
- **Hardware:** ddrum — signed alongside the drum endorsement
- **Notable:** Vinnie Paul formed Hellyeah in 2006 and signed with ddrum in 2008, who built his Vinnie Paul Signature Series kit — his definitive and final setup. He continued with ddrum, Evans, and Sabian through Hellyeah's run until his passing on June 22, 2018.

## Key Gear Changes

- **1990:** Signed with Tama — Cowboys from Hell foundational setup; Sabian AA/AAX cymbal endorsement begins (lifelong partnership) alongside Vic Firth American Classic 5B sticks
- **1996:** Switched from Tama to Pearl drums, coinciding with The Great Southern Trendkill
- **2008:** Signed with ddrum (drums and hardware) and Evans (heads) for the Hellyeah era — his final endorsement deal, lasting until his death in 2018
- **Continuity:** Sabian cymbals and Vic Firth American Classic 5B sticks were never switched — both endorsements ran continuously from the 1990s through his final Hellyeah shows

## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Vinnie Paul use on Cowboys from Hell?**  
A: On Cowboys from Hell (1990), Vinnie Paul played a Tama kit, having just signed with Tama during Pantera's major-label breakthrough. He used Sabian AA/AAX cymbals and Vic Firth American Classic 5B sticks — both endorsements he kept for the rest of his career.

**Q: What drums did Vinnie Paul use on Far Beyond Driven?**  
A: For Far Beyond Driven (1994), Vinnie Paul was still playing Tama drums — the switch to Pearl didn't happen until 1996, with The Great Southern Trendkill. His Sabian cymbals and Vic Firth American Classic 5B sticks were unchanged.

**Q: When did Vinnie Paul switch from Tama to Pearl?**  
A: Vinnie Paul switched from Tama to Pearl drums in 1996, around The Great Southern Trendkill. He stayed on Pearl through Reinventing the Steel (2000) and Damageplan's New Found Power (2004), until signing with ddrum in 2008.

**Q: What cymbals did Vinnie Paul use?**  
A: Vinnie Paul used Sabian AA/AAX Series cymbals continuously from the early 1990s through his final Hellyeah shows in 2018 — he never switched cymbal brands.

**Q: What was Vinnie Paul's final drum kit?**  
A: Vinnie Paul's final setup was the ddrum Vinnie Paul Signature Series, which ddrum built for him after he signed in 2008. It was paired with Evans drumheads (also signed in 2008), ddrum hardware, Sabian AA/AAX cymbals, and Vic Firth American Classic 5B sticks — his longest-running endorsement, which lasted until his death in June 2018.
