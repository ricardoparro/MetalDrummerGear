## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit did Waltteri Väyrynen use on The Last Will and Testament?**
A: Waltteri Väyrynen used a Pearl Masters Maple-tier kit (22" bass drum, 10"/12" rack toms, 16" floor tom) with a Sabian 22" HH Power Bell Ride and Wincent W-5BXL sticks on Opeth's 2024 album The Last Will and Testament.

**Q: How much does Waltteri Väyrynen's drum setup cost?**
A: Väyrynen's Opeth-era rig cost approximately $3,913 at 2024 retail, equivalent to roughly $4,076 today after inflation.

**Q: What is Waltteri Väyrynen's favourite cymbal?**
A: Per Sabian's own artist page, Väyrynen's favourite piece is the 22" HH Power Bell Ride — the rest of his cymbal lineup isn't itemized on Sabian's materials.

**Q: Does Waltteri Väyrynen have signature drumsticks?**
A: Yes — Väyrynen plays the Wincent W-5BXL, a hickory, extra-long, acorn-tip signature model announced through Wincent's artist roster in December 2022, shortly after he joined Opeth.

**Q: Was Waltteri Väyrynen in Paradise Lost before Opeth?**
A: Yes — Väyrynen was a session drummer for Paradise Lost starting in 2015 and an official member from 2016 until his September 2022 departure, recording "Medusa" (2017) and "Obsidian" (2020) before joining Opeth.
