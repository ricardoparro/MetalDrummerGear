## FAQ

**Q: What drum kit does Waltteri Väyrynen play?**
A: Waltteri Väyrynen is an endorsed Pearl Drums artist, a relationship confirmed via Pearl's own artist roster and corroborated by Antelope Audio's 2020 writeup of his studio session at Orgone Studios. Specific shell sizes aren't independently documented since Pearl's artist page blocks automated access, so Pearl's flagship Masters Maple line is used here as the representative professional-tier model.

**Q: How much does Waltteri Väyrynen's drum setup cost?**
A: Väyrynen's documented The Last Will and Testament-era (2024) setup cost approximately $3,913 at retail, equivalent to roughly $4,076 today after inflation.

**Q: What cymbals does Waltteri Väyrynen use?**
A: Sabian's own artist page names a 22" HH Power Bell Ride as Väyrynen's favourite cymbal in the setup. The rest of his cymbal lineup isn't itemized on Sabian's materials, so only this confirmed piece is priced.

**Q: What drumsticks does Waltteri Väyrynen use?**
A: Väyrynen plays the Wincent W-5BXL, a hickory extra-long model with an acorn tip, announced as his signature stick through Wincent's artist roster in December 2022, shortly after he joined Opeth.
