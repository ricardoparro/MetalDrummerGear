# Metal Tempo by Subgenre: How Fast Is Death Metal, Really?

> **Last updated:** 2026-07-25 · **Source:** [MetalForge.io](https://metalforge.io) · [View full study →](https://metalforge.io/studies/metal-tempo-by-subgenre)

---

## Overview

Average, median, and max BPM for thrash, death, black, and 12 other metal subgenres, computed across the 150 songs in MetalForge’s tempo database, plus the 200+ BPM "hall of speed."

---

## Headline Finding

Across the 254 songs in MetalForge's tempo database, death metal averages 176.9 BPM, well above the all-genre average of 141.1 BPM.

## Methodology

Dataset: 254 songs in MetalForge's tempo database, snapshot dated 2026-07-25. Overall average across all songs: 141.1 BPM (median 140 BPM). BPMs in this dataset are approximate — sourced from common tempo markings, not per-song verified audio analysis; treat exact figures as indicative, not laboratory-precise. Subgenre buckets follow each song's primary genre tag; songs spanning multiple subgenres are assigned to their most commonly cited genre.
