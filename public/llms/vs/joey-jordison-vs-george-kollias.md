## Playing Style

Joey brought death metal blast beat speed and theatrical intensity to Slipknot's nu-metal aggression. George built his reputation on sustained, unrelenting blast beats within Nile's Egyptian-themed technical death metal.

## Technique

Joey combined blast beats with groove and showmanship, playing at extreme tempos while masked and elevated on a rotating riser. George uses heel-toe double bass technique to sustain blast beats far longer than most drummers, a skill he has documented in his own instructional books.

Joey proved extreme metal technicality could exist inside a mainstream nu-metal act, influencing a generation of hybrid metal drummers. George raised the technical ceiling for sustained blast beats in death metal and became a leading voice in modern drum education.
