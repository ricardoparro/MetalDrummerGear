## Playing Style

Shannon Larkin, born in 1967 in Chicago, became Godsmack's longtime drummer in 2002, driving the band's hard rock sound across multi-platinum albums including "Faceless" (2003), "IV" (2006), and "The Oracle" (2010) before departing in 2024, after earlier stints with Ugly Kid Joe, Wrathchild America, Amen, and Candlebox. Mario Duplantier co-founded progressive death metal band Gojira with his brother Joe Duplantier in Bayonne, France, in 1996, building crushing double bass patterns layered with tribal, polyrhythmic structures across albums like "From Mars to Sirius" (2005) and "Magma" (2016).

## Technique

Larkin's technique is built for solid, powerful grooves and dynamic fills rather than technical flash, giving Godsmack's riffs their driving, radio-ready hard rock weight across two decades of multi-platinum records. Duplantier's technique blends raw power with polyrhythmic complexity and a tribal, ceremonial quality, locking into Gojira's tuned, resonant guitar tones with explosive fills and odd-meter grooves that push progressive death metal's tuned-percussion attack forward.
